## Purpose

This directory holds **Letter of Understanding (LOU)** instances for the Constitutional Engineering Office Engineering Definition phase.

Path: `Engineering-Office/definition/`

---

## LOU Naming

- Form: `LOU-NNN`
- Sequential numbering
- Numbers are never reused
- Filename convention: `LOU-NNN-Short-Title.md`
- Structure governed by [TMP-002](../templates/TMP-002-Letter-of-Understanding-Template.md)

---

## Human Engineer Acceptance

An LOU becomes accepted engineering understanding only after **explicit Human Engineer acceptance** (WF-001 HG-D1).

**Silence is not acceptance.**

---

## Research Non-Authority

Research recorded in an LOU Research Record / Evidence Annex is **informative, not authoritative**.

AI research does not create engineering authority.  
Classification of sources does not confer engineering authority.

---

## LOU Does Not Authorize Implementation

**THIS LOU RECORDS ACCEPTED ENGINEERING UNDERSTANDING.**  
**IT DOES NOT AUTHORIZE IMPLEMENTATION.**

Implementation requires a separately approved CWC-CE and all applicable downstream controls.

---

## SPEC Transition

After LOU acceptance, structured Requirements / Scope Definition preferentially use **SPEC** (`SPEC-NNN`) or an approved equivalent (STD-001).

No parallel REQ series is used.

Requirements/SPEC acceptance (HG-D2) also does **not** authorize implementation.

---

## CWC Transition

Controlled Execution begins with an approved **CWC-CE**.  
When Engineering Definition applies, the CWC-CE cites accepted LOU and SPEC (or records an explicit Human Engineer waiver for trivial/corrective work).

---

## Instances

No `LOU-NNN` instance is created by this directory README alone.  
LOU instances are created only under authorized CWC work.
