# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Letter of Understanding is to place in writing the overarching Engineering Definition of the Constitutional Engineering Office program before subordinate domains are fully engineered.

The overarching engineering objective is:

**Measure the current republic against the accepted Target Structured Republic, identify the gaps, engineer controlled means of closing those gaps, verify closure, identify residual gaps, and prevent reintroduction through drift.**

This is an **accepted Master** Engineering Definition artifact at HG-D1 (Version Draft 0.5).

It does **not** create, renumber, modify, or supersede existing subordinate LOUs (including LOU-001).

# 3. Human Engineering Intent

The Human Engineer intends that the Constitutional Engineering Office engineer toward an accepted **Target Structured Republic** using disciplined, checklist-based measurement of the present governmental condition against accepted target conditions.

The Human Engineer intends that the Target Structured Republic operate as more than a static collection of constitutional principles. Where properly authorized and engineered, accepted constitutional and fiscal controls should be capable of producing continuing, observable, traceable, and verifiable consequences as government operates — a **Constitutional Runtime**, including a candidate **Constitutional Fiscal Runtime**.

The Human Engineer intends that material gaps be verified, defined, authorized, closed, and re-verified through the existing Engineering Definition and Controlled Execution lifecycle — not by declaration, popularity, AI synthesis, or one-time legislative events alone.

The Human Engineer intends that **Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA)** serve as a candidate continuous operating discipline for gap closure and maintenance of the Target Structured Republic, working **through** (not replacing) the existing controlled lifecycle.

The Human Engineer intends that CHECK seek **Clear Signals of Reality**, distinguishing activity from outcome, and that every future legislative product engineered by the Constitutional Engineering Office include or be paired with an explicit CHECK concept and a defined pathway to ACT.

The Human Engineer intends that taxpayers ultimately have practical digital access to a trustworthy **Constitutional Digital Twin** of the constitutional governmental system — including a long-term accessibility objective often expressed as a digital twin of the Constitution on every taxpayer's phone — and that **tax-payment telemetry** eventually make relevant tax-resource flows observable and traceable as fiscal telemetry (not citizen surveillance), integrated with Open APIs, public ledgers, NBBF, Constitutional Runtime / Fiscal Runtime, Checklist for Liberty, Clear Signals of Reality, and PDCA.

# 8. Gap Closure

A gap closes only when:

1. accepted closure requirements exist (after applicable HG-D2 / Requirements/SPEC acceptance, as required by Active controls); **and**
2. Controlled Execution is authorized by approved CWC-CE (and ECR when required); **and**
3. implementation occurs within authorized scope; **and**
4. **CHECK** against accepted target/closure requirements using objective evidence demonstrates that the accepted target/closure condition has been achieved.

**A GAP IS NOT CLOSED BECAUSE SOMEONE DECLARES IT CLOSED.**

A gap shall **not** be considered closed merely because:

- a bill passed;
- an appropriation was made;
- an agency acted;
- a system was deployed;
- a deadline was met; or
- a report was produced.

Those facts may be valid **activity / implementation** evidence. They are not necessarily proof that the accepted Target State was achieved.

Where CHECK demonstrates failure or incomplete closure, **ACT** must have a controlled pathway.

Candidate CHECK/ACT result classifications (conceptual; use Active STD-001 / WF-001 terminology where it already controls):

- CLOSED  
- RESIDUAL GAP  
- REOPENED GAP  
- INDETERMINATE / EVIDENCE GAP  
- NEW ENGINEERING DEFINITION REQUIRED

# 9. Residual Gaps

If the target condition has not been fully achieved after implementation and verification, the remaining difference shall be identified as a:

**RESIDUAL GAP**

and returned to the engineering cycle.

Residual gaps remain visible. They shall not be erased by narrative success claims.

# 10. Drift / Reopening

**DRIFT** is a continuing engineering concern.

A gap that was once closed may reappear. An accepted system may be altered. Authority may expand. Temporary mechanisms may become permanent. Data visibility may degrade. Debt may accumulate. Exceptions may consume the rule. Implementation may diverge from accepted requirements.

Therefore the Structured Republic shall be treated as a **maintained configuration**, not a one-time legislative event.

The Checklist for Liberty should ultimately support recurring evaluation against the accepted Target Structured Republic.

**No recurrence schedule is defined under this Draft 0.3.** Exact legislative-calendar or PDCA recurrence mechanics remain deferred (see Legislative Runtime / Open Questions).

Candidate reopening/drift states remain conceptual until later controlled work defines authoritative terminology.

# 11. Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA)

**PLAN-DO-CHECK-ACT (PDCA)** is recorded as a **candidate program-level operating discipline** for continuous gap closure and maintenance of the Target Structured Republic.

This Master LOU describes PDCA at conceptual level only. It does **not** create production PDCA requirements, automatic triggers, metrics, or schedules under this CWC.

### 10.1 PLAN

PLAN (conceptually):

- establish the accepted Target State;
- establish Current State using evidence;
- identify and verify the gap;
- identify applicable authority and controls;
- define the intended closure condition;
- develop and accept Engineering Definition;
- develop and accept Requirements/SPEC where required;
- authorize Controlled Execution through the applicable CWC-CE lifecycle.

### 10.2 DO

DO (conceptually):

- execute only the authorized change;
- preserve scope and authority boundaries;
- generate truthful implementation evidence;
- do **not** treat implementation activity as proof of success.

### 10.3 CHECK

CHECK (conceptually):

- measure actual results against the accepted target and requirements;
- seek **Clear Signals of Reality**;
- use objective verification evidence;
- determine whether the target condition was actually achieved;
- identify failure, unintended consequences, residual gaps, reopened gaps, or drift where present.

### 10.4 ACT

ACT (conceptually):

- provide a defined response pathway based on CHECK results;
- preserve successful conditions where appropriate;
- correct failed implementation;
- address residual gaps;
- reopen closed gaps when evidence demonstrates recurrence;
- initiate new Engineering Definition where necessary;
- apply continuation, renewal, expiration, reduction, corrective action, or termination only where authorized by accepted controls and law.

# 13. Clear Signals of Reality

**CLEAR SIGNALS OF REALITY** is an essential characteristic of CHECK.

The Master LOU distinguishes:

**ACTIVITY SIGNALS**

from:

**REALITY / OUTCOME SIGNALS.**

Activity signals may include, conceptually:

- a bill was introduced;
- legislation passed;
- money was appropriated;
- a program was created;
- an agency submitted a report;
- software was deployed;
- a review meeting occurred.

These facts may be valid implementation evidence.

They are **not** necessarily proof that the accepted Target State was achieved.

CHECK should seek objective evidence capable of answering:

**DID THE ENGINEERED CHANGE ACTUALLY PRODUCE THE ACCEPTED RESULT?**

Principles:

**ACTIVITY IS NOT THE SAME AS OUTCOME.**

**IMPLEMENTATION IS NOT THE SAME AS VERIFIED GAP CLOSURE.**
