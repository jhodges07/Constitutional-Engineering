## 1. Executive finding

Kansas currently imposes compulsory governmental payment claims that function, in whole or in part, as **fees, licenses, permits, registrations, filings, certifications, inspections, franchises, or regulatory charges**. The government **label** is not dispositive. Kansas judicial doctrine treats the distinction between a fee and a tax as a question of **nature and function**, not the name used (*Executive Aircraft Consulting, Inc. v. City of Newton*, 252 Kan. 421 (1993); *Heartland Apartment Ass'n v. City of Mission*, 305 Kan. 250, 382 P.3d 308 (2016) — transportation utility fee held a **tax**). This CWC **does not** re-decide those holdings for every identified claim. Where function is mixed or destination includes general revenue: **LEGAL INTERPRETATION REQUIRED**.

## 2. Method and discovery principle

Audit by legal authority + trigger + obligated party + amount method + purpose + destination + renewal + consequence + economic function — **not** merely by the label “fee.”

A claim belongs in Domain 05 when evidence establishes that its **principal function** is a compulsory payment for license, permit, registration, title, franchise, regulatory authorization, application/filing, certification, inspection, or a materially equivalent regulatory claim.

Do **not**:

- pull Domain 01–04 taxes into Domain 05 merely because they are collected with a license;
- pull Domain 07 fines into Domain 05 merely because they appear on a court receipt;
- pull Domain 08 enterprise/user-pay receipts into Domain 05 merely because they are called fees;
- create a row for every profession, board, vehicle class, permit form, municipality, or inspection;
- treat collection by a county treasurer, marketplace seller, or clerk as a second claim.

PRIMARY-LEGAL and GOV-DATA remain distinguishable. AI/search results are discovery aids only. Introduced bills are not current law.

**Compulsory vs voluntary:** Domain 05 concerns claims that are compulsory **once the person chooses or enters the regulated activity/status**. Paying for an optional extra copy of a record is not automatically Domain 05. Kansas concealed-carry **permits remaining available after permitless carry** are treated as **optional / not a compulsory condition of legal carry in Kansas** and are **not** a counted Domain 05 row (WD-BILL-A-064 UNK-D05-009).

## 3. Motor-vehicle title / registration / driver architecture

A person encountering a vehicle in Kansas may face **multiple distinct claims**: Domain 01 motor-fuel (KRU-D01-001–003); Domain 02 motor-vehicle property / in-lieu tax (KRU-D02-012); Domain 04 sales/use on the vehicle; Domain 05 registration, title, and driver/CDL fees. They SHALL NOT be collapsed.

### 3.1 Registration (KRU-D05-001)

K.S.A. **8-143** imposes **annual** registration / license fees by vehicle class (examples: passenger ≤4,500 lb **$30**; >4,500 lb **$40**; all-electric passenger **$165**; trucks by declared gross weight up to **$2,070**). K.S.A. **8-145**: fees paid to the division of vehicles, a contractor, or the county treasurer; county treasurer retains an administrative portion; remainder remitted; destinations include the **state highway fund**, commercial-vehicle administrative fund, VIPS/CAMA technology hardware fund, Kansas highway patrol staffing and training fund, and specified electric/hybrid remittances via **79-34,142** percentages to SHF and the special city and county highway fund.

**Recurrence:** ANNUAL (with stated exceptions). **Cost-recovery class:** **MIXED** — statutory amounts are fixed schedules, not an evidenced per-vehicle administrative-cost formula; a substantial destination is the **state highway fund** (transportation program, not merely DMV counter cost). **FEE ≠ AUTOMATICALLY COST RECOVERY.**

Kansas ACFR FY2024 Transportation Fund, budgetary basis: **Vehicle registrations and permits $246,997 thousand** (expressed in thousands). That line **aggregates** registration and permit receipts credited to that fund presentation. It is **not** a replacement-revenue target and is **not** the sum of all Domain 05 claims.

### 3.2 Title (KRU-D05-002)

K.S.A. **8-135(c)(4)**: original certificate of title fee **$10** in addition to registration; title is good for the life of the vehicle while held by the original holder. Additional $10 on assignment/new title; other listed service amounts ($1.50, $2.50, $3, $6.50) are **mechanisms of the same title architecture**, not extra counted claims. **Recurrence:** EVENT-TRIGGERED (transfer / original issue). Nonpayment / operating without title: unlawful operation / fraudulent-and-void sale architecture in 8-135.

### 3.3 Driver / CDL (KRU-D05-003)

K.S.A. **8-240(f)** sets classified license fees (e.g. class C age 21–64 **$18**; CDL **$18**; endorsements **$10** except air brake). Examination fees $3 / $15 CDL drive test. K.S.A. **8-267** splits receipts among state safety fund, motorcycle safety fund, truck driver training fund, photo fee fund, hazmat fee fund, division of vehicles operating fund, correctional services special revenue fund, and **balance to the state highway fund**. **Recurrence:** PERIODIC (license term; not re-audited here as a single statewide term). **Cost-recovery class:** **MIXED** (driver-training and photo funds plus SHF remainder).

Vehicle **dealer** licensing (K.S.A. 8-2401 et seq.) is identified as a related distinct architecture. Exact current dealer-fee schedule was **not** fully retrieved this CWC: **UNK-D05-001**. Not a counted extra row.

## 4. Professional / occupational licensing (KRU-D05-004)

Kansas requires payment for legal authority to practice numerous professions through **separate boards and fee funds**. This CWC does **not** inventory every profession.

**Material architecture (evidenced):**

```text
STATUTE AUTHORIZING A BOARD / AGENCY TO LICENSE
→ APPLICATION / EXAMINATION / LICENSE / RENEWAL FEE
→ AGENCY FEE FUND
→ 10% CREDIT TO STATE GENERAL FUND (K.S.A. 75-3170a, where listed)
   UNTIL $100,000 PER FEE FUND PER FISCAL YEAR
→ REMAINDER TO THE FEE FUND
```

K.S.A. **75-3170a** states the 10% SGF credit “is to reimburse the state general fund for accounting, auditing, budgeting, legal, payroll, personnel and purchasing services, and any and all other state governmental services.” Listed fee statutes include, among others: 65-2855 (healing arts), 65-1817a (barbers), 74-1108 (nursing), 74-1405, 74-1503, 74-1609 (pharmacy), 58-3074 (real estate), 1-204 (accountancy), 74-7009 (technical professions).

**Cost-recovery class:** **MIXED**. The 10% SGF credit is an **express general-fund reimbursement** from regulatory receipts. That does **not**, by itself, prove the remaining 90% equals administrative cost, and does **not** prove the 10% is a tax. **LEGAL INTERPRETATION REQUIRED** as to fee-vs-tax if any particular board’s total charge “unreasonably exceeds” the regulatory service (*Executive Aircraft* quotation of federal cable-fee cases). No board-by-board cost study was performed.

**Recurrence:** typically ANNUAL or BIENNIAL — **LOCAL TO EACH BOARD** / **EVIDENCE REQUIRED** for a uniform statewide term.

## 5. Local occupation / business licenses (KRU-D05-005)

Cities exercise home-rule power (Kan. Const. art. **12, § 5**) and commonly require **occupation tax / business licenses** as a condition of operating inside city limits. Representative implementation (not statewide universal imposition): Unified Government of Wyandotte County / Kansas City, Kansas publishes that operating a business requires an **occupation tax license** (annual calendar year). K.S.A. **12-1617** (search-located; full text not the completeness bottleneck) restricts cities from certain occupation taxes on in-state agricultural producers — a **limitation**, not a claim.

**LOCAL IMPLEMENTATION VARIABLE.** One municipality ≠ one claim. Amounts, coverage, and whether the charge is regulatory vs revenue: **EVIDENCE REQUIRED** per jurisdiction; statewide function **LEGAL INTERPRETATION REQUIRED**.

## 11. 911 / telecommunications (KRU-D05-011)

Referred from Domain 04. **Not** a sales tax.

**Mechanism A — monthly 911 fee:** K.S.A. **12-5369(a)** imposes **$0.90 per month per subscriber account** of exchange telecommunications, wireless, VoIP, or other service capable of contacting a PSAP. **Not** imposed on prepaid wireless. Providers remit (12-5370). Board **may lower** the fee if moneys exceed costs required to operate PSAPs (12-5369(b)). Authorized uses: 12-5375.

**Mechanism B — prepaid wireless 911 fee:** K.S.A. **12-5371** imposes **2.06% per retail transaction**, collected by the seller from the consumer; remitted to KDOR consistent with article 36 of chapter 79 (collection **mechanism**, not a Domain 04 claim). History through L. 2024, ch. 53; July 1, 2025.

**Cost-recovery class:** monthly fee has an express **cost-to-operate-PSAPs** reduction authority → **REGULATORY / COST-RECOVERY CANDIDATE**. Prepaid percentage-of-transaction architecture is **MIXED**; whether it is a fee, surcharge, or tax: **LEGAL INTERPRETATION REQUIRED**. Seller collection ≠ a second claim.
