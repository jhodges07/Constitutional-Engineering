## A. Executive Domain 03 finding

**WHAT INCOME / EARNINGS / PRIVILEGE COMPULSORY GOVERNMENTAL CLAIMS CURRENTLY EXIST IN KANSAS?**

Kansas currently imposes **state individual income tax** (including fiduciary estates/trusts as a taxpayer class of the same Act), **state corporate income tax** (normal tax plus surtax), a **distinct financial-institution privilege tax measured by net income**, an **elective pass-through entity tax** when an S corporation or partnership elects entity-level taxation, and a **local-option tax on gross earnings from money, notes, and other evidence of debt** (K.S.A. 12-1,101) where a county, city, or township has imposed it.

Cities are **prohibited** from levying general income taxes except as specifically authorized by 12-1,101 through 12-1,109 (K.S.A. 12-140). Counties **may not** levy and collect taxes on incomes except as specifically authorized by 12-1,101 through 12-1,109 (K.S.A. 19-101a(a)(12)). Introduced local earnings-tax bills are **not current law**.

Withholding and estimated payments are **collection / prepayment mechanisms**, not distinct Domain 03 revenue claims.

This is a **current-state existence** answer from PRIMARY-LEGAL / GOV-DATA. It is **not** a Bill A RETAIN, TRANSFORM, or DISAPPEAR decision. Current receipts are **not** a required FairTax replacement amount.

## C. Individual income tax

**Authority:** Kan. Const. art. 11, § 2 (state **power** to levy and collect taxes on incomes; may be graduated and progressive — **AUTHORITY VERIFIED**, not a mandate to retain). K.S.A. 79-32,110(a)–(b), (d); definitions 79-32,109; Kansas taxable income of an individual = Kansas AGI less Kansas deductions and personal exemptions (79-32,116). Kansas AGI is built from federal AGI with Kansas modifications (79-32,117 — **not every modification copied**).

**Current rates (tax year 2024 and all tax years thereafter, unless later modified under 79-32,110c):**

Married filing jointly: 5.2% of Kansas taxable income not over $46,000; $2,392 plus 5.58% of excess over $46,000.  
All other individuals (and fiduciaries under (d)): 5.2% not over $23,000; $1,196 plus 5.58% of excess over $23,000.

**Nonresidents:** tax computed as if resident under (a), multiplied by the ratio of **modified Kansas source income** to Kansas AGI (79-32,110(b); source rules in 79-32,109(h)). Part-year / domicile rules sit in 79-32,109(b) (domicile; >6 months presumption). Not a separate claim.

**Fiduciaries:** estates and trusts taxed at (a)(2) rates (79-32,110(d)). Same Act, same destination. Not fragmented into a second row.

**Trigger:** Kansas taxable income of a resident individual; or the sourced computation for a nonresident; or Kansas taxable income of an estate/trust. Legal taxpayer = the individual / fiduciary. Economic incidence **NOT ESTABLISHED**. Ultimate economic source remains Human intent: people with money.

**Collection:** employer withholding (79-3294 / 79-3296) and estimated tax (79-32,101) are **mechanisms**. Destination: SGF after IMPACT credits and income-tax refund fund (79-32,105).

**Contingent future cuts:** L. 2025, ch. 116 / 79-32,110c. KDOR Notice 25-06: **no rate reduction for tax year 2026**. Do not treat the contingency as a current rate change.

**Fiscal scale (taxes levied for collection / Tax Facts; thousands):** FY2024 Individual **$4,523,616**; FY2025 Individual **$4,695,736**. DoA SGF FY2024 individual **$4,503,615,413**. Tax Facts **Table 4 Other Funds** ~$20 million: CWC-CE-134 **RESOLVED — ACCOUNTING CLASSIFICATION** (IMPACT / 74-50,107 job-creation cap; not local income tax). See WD-BILL-A-046.

## D. Withholding / estimated payments

**Withholding:** K.S.A. 79-3294 titles the “Kansas withholding and declaration of estimated tax act.” 79-3296(a): every employer required to withhold federally shall withhold from wages of a Kansas resident or wages for personal service performed in Kansas, in an amount determined under 79-32,100d. Definitions 79-3295 (employee, employer, wages = IRC §3401(a) wages taxable under the Kansas income tax act).

**Classification:** **COLLECTION / PREPAYMENT MECHANISM** for KRU-D03-001 (and related income-tax act liabilities). **Not a distinct Domain 03 claim.** Do not add withholding dollars to individual income-tax receipts.

**Estimated tax:** 79-32,101 — individuals expecting to owe ≥$500 after withholding/credits with withholding/credits below specified safe harbors; corporations expecting Kansas income-tax liability >$500. **PAYMENT MECHANISM**, not a distinct claim. Estates/trusts excluded from 79-32,101(e).

## E. Corporate income tax

**Authority:** 79-32,110(c). Tax on Kansas taxable income of every corporation doing business in Kansas or deriving income from sources in Kansas. **Normal tax 4%** of Kansas taxable income **plus surtax 3%** of Kansas taxable income in excess of **$50,000**, unless modified under 74-50,321 or 79-32,110c.

**Base:** federal taxable income with Kansas addition/subtraction modifications (79-32,138). If income is not entirely from Kansas sources, allocation/apportionment under the Uniform Division of Income for Tax Purposes Act (79-3271 through 79-3293) — **structural only; not a treatise**.

**Legal taxpayer:** the corporation. Remitter: the corporation (estimated tax 79-32,101(a)(2)). Destination: SGF (79-32,105).

**Fiscal scale (Tax Facts thousands):** FY2024 Corporation **$1,419,201**; FY2025 Corporation **$1,313,558**. DoA SGF FY2024 corporation **$1,419,200,508**.

**Relationship to privilege tax:** K.S.A. **79-32,113(c)** (CWC-CE-134) exempts insurance companies, banks, trust companies, savings and loan associations, and credit unions from the Kansas Income Tax Act. 79-1106 “in lieu of” remains **ad valorem on intangibles**. Classification: **distinct claims** (CF-D03-002 **RESOLVED — DIFFERENT LEGAL CLAIMS**). Federally chartered savings-bank naming vs 79-32,113(c): residual **LEGAL INTERPRETATION REQUIRED**. Do not double-count fiscal lines. Credit unions: income-tax exempt, **not** listed in 79-1106 — **not** a Domain 03 privilege row. Insurance: premium tax, not Domain 03.

## G. Pass-through / entity treatment

**Default:** S corporations and partnerships are generally not the income-tax taxpayer; owners report distributive shares (79-32,129 / 79-32,139 architecture; election in 79-32,286 is “notwithstanding” those sections).

**Elective entity-level tax:** 79-32,286 — for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2022, an S corporation or partnership **may annually elect** to be taxed at the entity level. Binding on electing owners.

**Rate:** highest individual rate under 79-32,110(a) for that year (79-32,287(a)). For TY 2024+ that highest rate is **5.58%** (KDOR SALT Parity FAQ / current 79-32,110(a) schedules).

**Base:** nonresident owners — Kansas-attributable share; resident owners — either (Kansas + non-Kansas) or Kansas-only, applied consistently (79-32,287(a)).

**Taxpayer when elected:** the electing pass-through entity (79-32,287(e)). Owner architecture **79-32,288 retrieved** (CWC-CE-134): owners not liable in a separate capacity; credit = owner’s direct share of 79-32,286 tax; excess **refundable**; basis as if election not made. **Do not add PTE receipts to individual receipts as if they were additional net revenue.** Isolated statewide **actual** PTE dollars: still **EVIDENCE REQUIRED** (UNK-D03-003). CRE Long Memo treats SALT Parity as **net-neutral shift** (estimate, not isolated actual).

KRU-D03-004 is a **distinct claim** because the entity is the statutory taxpayer when the election is in force. It is **not** a second tax on the same dollar after the owner credit if the credit operates as designed; fiscal netting is **AGGREGATED** in official income-tax lines.

## H. K.S.A. 12-1,101 intangibles referral resolution

**DOMAIN 03 VERIFIED CLAIM** — **AUTHORITY EXISTS BUT LOCAL IMPLEMENTATION VARIABLE.**

Not a state mill. Not classified tangible-property ad valorem (Domain 02 correctly referred it). It is a **local-option tax on gross earnings** derived from money, notes, and other evidence of debt (12-1,101; definitions 12-1,102).

| Level | Enabling cap | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| County | 1/8 of 1% up to 3/4 of 1% of total gross earnings | Resolution |
| City | 1/8 of 1% up to 2¼% | Ordinance |
| Township (outside third-class city limits) | 1/8 of 1% up to 2¼% | Resolution |

Compulsory **where imposed**. Enabling authority ≠ every jurisdiction levies. Petition/election paths exist to eliminate or impose (12-1,101(e)–(f)).

**Fiscal (Tax Facts FY2025 thousands, local intangibles):** counties **$1,310**; cities **$623**; townships **$512**; schools **$0**. Combined Table 1 intangibles **$2,445**. State intangibles column **$0**. Do not treat this as a statewide income tax.

**Implementation universe (CWC-CE-134):** Official statewide rate list is KDOR **Form 200** (`https://www.ksrevenue.gov/pdf/20025.pdf`, current to July 1, 2025). Full 105-county roster **not transcribed** here. **LOCAL IMPLEMENTATION INCOMPLETE** as a narrative inventory. Enabling ≠ every jurisdiction imposes.
