# 1. State tax-system conversion

The Kansas **state-level** tax architecture shall be converted to the H.R. 25-style consumption-tax architecture already selected as Bill A's economic tax-base reference.

The state **SHALL NOT** preserve a parallel traditional state income/payroll-tax system merely because that system exists in the Current State.

Not inferred: exact Kansas rates; taxable-base mechanics; administration; exemptions; distributions; statutory language beyond already-established H.R. 25 reference work.

H.R. 25 remains the Human-selected substantive economic reference for Bill A's Future-State consumption-tax architecture, including final consumption; business/investment-purpose; resale/production; used-property; mixed-use; gaming architecture; and EXISTENCE IS NOT A TAXABLE EVENT. NBBF does not modify that tax base.

ALL-IN counties participate in the new Bill A state/local tax architecture (H.R. 25-style consumption-tax architecture at the applicable local envelope as later engineered).

CURRENT STATE → TRR TRANSITION → ARR FUTURE STATE

# 3. ALL-OUT county architecture

An ALL-OUT county does **not** participate in the new **local** H.R. 25-style replacement architecture.

An ALL-OUT county:

A. receives **no** Bill A replacement-revenue guarantee;  
B. remains subject to statewide mandatory property-tax elimination;  
C. remains subject to statewide taxpayer-control, transparency, accountability, API, and Runtime Republic architecture;  
D. may operate only those local taxes, fees, or other governmental claims that Bill A ultimately **authorizes to survive** the conversion;  
E. may establish its own initial rates or amounts for authorized surviving claims, **subject to Government Claim Degradation**;  
F. may operate a traditional **personal** income/payroll-tax system within the county jurisdiction;  
G. may operate a traditional **business** income/payroll-tax system within the county jurisdiction;  
H. **SHALL NOT** impose an inheritance tax.

ALL-OUT is **not** authority to revive a claim eliminated by Bill A.

**Reconciliation with CWC-CE-142:** The four rules remain controlling. “ALL-OUT = legacy tax system minus property tax” is **no longer a complete description** of ALL-OUT revenue architecture. CWC-CE-155 **refines** ALL-OUT: not automatic retention of every current local claim; no new local H.R. 25 replacement participation; authorized surviving claims only; county income/payroll **may** be operated within jurisdictional bounds; inheritance tax prohibited; Government Claim Degradation applies to authorized surviving compulsory claims. Recorded as CF-BILL-A-016. CWC-142 four rules are **not superseded**.

TRR is **not** inferred to apply to ALL-OUT as a Bill A replacement guarantee.

# 4. ALL-OUT county income/payroll jurisdiction

County income/payroll-tax authority is jurisdictionally bounded.

Temporary physical presence in a county does **not** by itself establish county income-tax jurisdiction.

A person temporarily working within the county, including TDY-type temporary duty, shall **not** become subject to the county's income/payroll claim solely because of that temporary presence.

For the business/work-location case described by the Human Engineer, **both**:

1. the business; **and**  
2. the work location  

must **originate within the county** before the ALL-OUT county may impose the applicable local income/payroll claim.

**Not invented** (UQ-CTS-017): residence; domicile; originate; business origin; work-location origin; TDY; temporary presence; payroll nexus; income nexus; remote work; multi-county employment; business entity residence.

# 6. Government Claim Degradation

**ESTABLISHED** as a controlled Bill A Definition concept.

Any compulsory monetary claim by government against a citizen, person, household, taxpayer, business, property owner, or other governed party must contain an authorized degradation path over time unless it falls within the Human-authorized **jury-decision exception**.

Government **SHALL NOT** evade degradation by changing the name, classification, collection mechanism, governmental recipient, accounting treatment, or administrative form of the claim.

```text
SUBSTANCE CONTROLS OVER LABEL.
```

Illustrative labels (not an authorization for any claim to survive): tax; fee; assessment; levy; surcharge; license charge; permit charge; compulsory contribution; mandatory payment; governmental charge; other compulsory monetary demand.

---

# 7. Authorization before degradation

```text
DEGRADATION DOES NOT AUTHORIZE A CLAIM.
CURRENT CLAIM ≠ AUTHORIZED SURVIVING CLAIM.
DEGRADING CLAIM ≠ AUTHORIZED CLAIM.
```

A claim must first survive the applicable Bill A authorization/classification process. Only then does its required degradation path become relevant. A prohibited claim does not become lawful merely because its amount declines.

This CWC **SHALL NOT** assign Domain 01–06 Field 25 dispositions. Universe **NOT CERTIFIED**. KLRS **NOT CERTIFIED**. Count **72 arithmetic only**.

# 8. Percentage-based claim degradation

A percentage-based surviving compulsory government claim subject to degradation declines automatically by:

**0.25 PERCENTAGE POINTS EVERY THREE MONTHS**  
→ **1.00 PERCENTAGE-POINT REDUCTION OVER A FULL YEAR.**

Example (illustrative only):

| Date | Rate |
|---|---|
| January Year 1 | 10.00% |
| April Year 1 | 9.75% |
| July Year 1 | 9.50% |
| October Year 1 | 9.25% |
| January Year 2 | 9.00% |

This is a **percentage-POINT** reduction. It is **not** a 25% quarterly reduction, a 0.25% proportional reduction, or a compounded percentage reduction.

---

# 9. Annual average-rate rule

Where liability is determined on an annual basis while the applicable percentage rate degrades during that year, annual liability shall use the **arithmetic average** of the applicable rates for that annual period.

Example only: (10.00 + 9.75 + 9.50 + 9.25) / 4 = **9.625%**.

**Not inferred** (UQ-GCD-005): partial years; non-calendar fiscal years; irregular reporting periods; mid-quarter starts; mid-quarter termination; delinquency; amended returns; rounding.

---

# 10. Fixed-dollar claim degradation

Fixed-dollar compulsory claims shall degrade by:

**1% OF THE ORIGINAL STARTING AMOUNT PER FULL YEAR.**

The reduction is based upon the **ORIGINAL STARTING AMOUNT**. It **SHALL NOT** compound from the declining balance unless the Human Engineer later changes this rule.

Example: original hunting fee $10.00 → Start $10.00; Year 1 $9.90; Year 2 $9.80; Year 3 $9.70.

**Not inferred** (UQ-GCD-002): quarterly fixed-dollar implementation; rounding; minimum collectible amount; terminal mechanics.

---

# 11. Claim-by-any-name

A compulsory governmental monetary demand is evaluated according to its **substantive effect** rather than its title. Renaming a tax as a fee, assessment, surcharge, license, permit, contribution, service charge, or another term does **not** remove the degradation requirement when the underlying substance remains a compulsory government claim.

Reconcile with Kansas Government Revenue Universe and KLRS. Do **not** prematurely classify all existing fees or enterprise/user charges as compulsory. Classification remains evidence-dependent (UQ-GCD-003). Option (a) KLRS = compulsory demand-authority only remains controlling for **closed post-Bill-A demand membership**. Degradation applies to **authorized surviving compulsory** claims; it does not convert voluntary receipts into KLRS claims.

---

# 12. Jury-decision exception

A monetary claim arising from a **court decision by a jury** is outside the general Government Claim Degradation requirement.

**Do not broaden.** **Not automatically included** (UQ-GCD-004): administrative penalties; agency determinations; executive assessments; regulatory penalties; non-jury judgments; settlements; consent decrees; fines imposed without a jury decision; governmental invoices.

No constitutional or statutory language drafted.

# 13. Enforceability

Except for the jury-decision exception, a compulsory government monetary claim that is required to degrade but does not contain and follow its authorized degradation path is **NOT ENFORCEABLE** under the intended Bill A architecture.

```text
GOVERNMENT CLAIM
→ LAWFUL AUTHORITY
→ CLEAR SIGNAL
→ AUTHORIZED CLAIM
→ REQUIRED DEGRADATION PATH
→ CONTINUING ENFORCEABILITY
→ ZERO OR AUTHORIZED TERMINAL CONDITION
```

Preserve: SIGNAL MUST PRECEDE CLAIM. NO AUTHORITY → NO VALID CLAIM. NO REQUIRED DEGRADATION → NO CONTINUING ENFORCEABILITY, subject to the jury-decision exception and later precise legal engineering.

**Not current Kansas law.** Terminal/zero condition mechanics remain **UQ-GCD-001**.
