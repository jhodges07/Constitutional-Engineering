# 2. Municipal fiscal architecture (controlled depth)

**20 cities** (WD-BILL-A-083).

**Wichita:** mill-sheet total city **32.340 mills** (General 25.335 + Bond & Interest 7.005). **No municipal sales tax** in the general Wichita combined rate of 7.5% (state 6.5 + county 1.0). Wichita **does** receive a **formula share** of the **countywide** 1% (K.S.A. 12-192). Human/secondary “nearly $7 million” is **not** verified as Wichita’s full countywide-1% share (WD-BILL-A-087).

Wichita also has **city property tax**, fees, enterprise utilities, assessments, CID/TDD — **EVIDENCE REQUIRED** for a Wichita ACFR-scale map in a later CWC.

**Other cities:** mill totals on 2025 sheet range roughly **126–173 mills** all-in (city+county+state+school+specials), e.g. Bentley 173.349; Viola 126.797. City-only mills vary widely (Viola general 21.918 vs Bentley 60.010).

Municipal sales-tax **city-imposed** rates: several Sedgwick cities may levy city RST under K.S.A. 12-187/12-189. **KS-1700 inventory EVIDENCE REQUIRED.** Wichita city rate **0%** is the important Current-State fact for the largest city.
