## 2. Referrals **without** executing later or prior domains

| Item | Referral | Why | Do not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motor-fuel / LP-gas / 24–72-hour fuel permits | Domain 01 (KRU-D01-001–003) | Excise / in-lieu fuel tax | Duplicate in D05 |
| Liquor gallonage / drink / enforcement | Domain 01 (KRU-D01-007–009) | Excise | Collapse into liquor license |
| Cigarette / OTP / e-cig excise | Domain 01 (KRU-D01-004–006) | Excise | Collapse into tobacco dealer license |
| Charitable-gaming bingo **taxes** | Domain 01 (KRU-D01-014) | Tax | Collapse into gaming **license** |
| Motor-vehicle property / in-lieu tax | Domain 02 (KRU-D02-012) | Property-based | Collapse into registration |
| Financial-institution privilege tax | Domain 03 (79-1107/1108) | Privilege tax | Collapse into OSBC fees |
| State/local retailers' sales and compensating use | Domain 04 | Consumption tax | Treat 911 KDOR remittance as RST |
| Insurance **premium tax** (40-252 tax lines) | Later domain / unclassified tax (not D05; not D03) | Tax on premiums | Domain 05 fee row |
| Special assessments / benefit districts / TUF-type taxes | **Domain 06** | Assessment or tax on property/geography/industry | Execute Domain 06 |
| Fines, late fees, interest, civil penalties, criminal fines | **Domain 07** | Punishment / noncompliance | Execute Domain 07 |
| Utility enterprise rates, park concessions, product/service user-pay | **Domain 08** | Purchase of a government product/service rather than legal permission | Execute Domain 08 |
| Intergovernmental grants | Domain 09 | Not a Domain 05 claim | Misclassify as a fee |
| Bond / borrowing proceeds | Domain 10 | Financing, not a regulatory claim | Misclassify as a fee |
| Optional record copies / expedited SOS souvenirs | Generally **non-compulsory** / voluntary | Not Domain 05 unless only path to legal status | Force into D05 |
| Concealed-carry **optional** permit after permitless carry | Not counted Domain 05 | Not a compulsory condition of legal carry in Kansas | Treat as required license |

## 4. H.R. 25 (federal model only)

Domain 05 claims are generally **outside** H.R. 25 final-consumption taxable events. Potential **collection stacking** exists where prepaid 911 is collected on a retail prepaid-wireless sale alongside Domain 04 sales tax. That is **not** a finding that 911 is H.R. 25.
