# 2. Engineering interpretation (not a Human quote)

**Classification:** ENGINEERING INTERPRETATION of selection A plus the additional Human statement.

Every authorized surviving compulsory governmental monetary claim subject to Government Claim Degradation continues along its authorized degradation path until the applicable compulsory **rate or amount reaches ZERO**. ZERO is the general terminal destination. Once at zero, that claim is no longer enforceable as a compulsory monetary demand.

No automatic permanent non-zero floor. Historical revenue, rates, fees, budgets, spending, convenience, expectations, or governmental preference are **not** authority to stop above zero. Any future exception requires separate Human authorization.

The sinking-fund statement is recorded as a **Future-State design objective**: personal and governmental sinking funds (two distinct categories) accumulating capital so authorized functions may increasingly be supported by earnings as compulsory claims degrade toward zero. It is **not** a finding that perpetual interest, any return, any balance, or any function-finance is proven. Cross-reference existing Q-BILL-A-001 / WD-BILL-A-007 §8 sinking-fund financial-discipline intent; this CWC **does not rewrite** that earlier intent.

UQ-GCD-001 is **answered in principle**. UQ-GCD-002 (quarterly / rounding / fractional-cent / last collectible increment) remains **OPEN** as later mechanics, not asked now.

# 3. Zero Terminal principle

```text
AUTHORIZED COMPULSORY CLAIM
→ REQUIRED DEGRADATION
→ CONTINUING REDUCTION
→ ZERO
→ COMPULSORY CLAIM TERMINATES.

DEGRADATION DOES NOT AUTHORIZE A CLAIM.
CLAIM AUTHORIZATION must precede DEGRADATION.
```

A prohibited claim does not become lawful because it degrades.

---

# 4. No automatic non-zero floor

```text
NO AUTOMATIC PERMANENT NON-ZERO FLOOR.
```

Government SHALL NOT treat historical revenue, historical tax rates, historical fee amounts, historical budgets, historical spending, administrative convenience, revenue expectations, or governmental preference as authority for stopping degradation above zero.

# 5. Percentage and fixed-dollar paths (preserved)

Percentage: **−0.25 percentage points every three months** = 1.00 percentage-point per year. Not proportional, not compounded. Continues toward ZERO.

Fixed-dollar: **1% of the original starting amount per full year**, not compounded from the declining balance. Continues toward ZERO.

UQ-GCD-002 / UQ-GCD-005 remain OPEN (mechanics). **Not resolved here.**

---

# 6. ZERO means ZERO

ZERO means the compulsory monetary claim has exhausted its authorized degradation path.

Government SHALL NOT automatically freeze immediately above zero, create a minimum permanent rate or fee, rename the residual, transfer the residual into another compulsory claim, or restart the claim under a different label.

Substance controls over label. A replacement compulsory claim requires independent Bill A authority.

---

# 7. Jury-decision exception (preserved; not broadened)

Only a monetary claim arising from a court decision by a jury is outside general GCD. Non-jury judgments, administrative penalties, agency determinations, regulatory penalties, settlements, consent decrees, governmental invoices, and other governmental demands are **not** automatically included. UQ-GCD-004 remains OPEN.
