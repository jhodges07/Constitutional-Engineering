# 3. Rejected inferences (this CWC)

Do **not** adopt:

1. That 5 or 7 years is a governmental entitlement to collect property tax for the full period.  
2. That STATE RATE = 5% or LOCAL RATE = 5%.  
3. That 5% + 5% is a guaranteed 10% tax.  
4. That county government owns the local 5%.  
5. That USDs are preassigned the lion's share.  
6. That historical collections equal future entitlement.  
7. That current revenue equals required replacement revenue.  
8. That younger owners pay seniors' former property tax (not authorized as the mechanism).  
9. That senior priority is a freeze, cap, deferral, rebate, partial exemption, or permanent separate property-tax class.  
10. That Sedgwick County results (when later audited) automatically become the statewide rate or template.  
11. That this CWC calculated any rate, replacement revenue, or distribution.  
12. That this CWC executed a Sedgwick fiscal audit.  
13. That this CWC assigned Domain 01–06 Human dispositions.  
14. That this CWC superseded CWC-CE-142 four-rule architecture.  
15. That this CWC is current Kansas law, HG-D1 acceptance, a SPEC, or legislation.
