## 2.1 Core transition rule

```text
LEGALLY OBLIGATED CURRENT SPENDING → TRANSITIONAL PROTECTION (TRR).
ALL NEW SPENDING → IMMEDIATE NBBF CONTROL.
```

This **replaces** any reading that all current lawful spending, all budgeted spending, or all historical programs automatically receive TRR protection.

```text
LEGAL OBLIGATION ≠ MERE HISTORICAL EXISTENCE.
LEGAL OBLIGATION ≠ CURRENT BUDGET LINE.
LEGAL OBLIGATION ≠ PERMANENT FUTURE-STATE ENTITLEMENT.
```

Do **not** silently broaden “legally obligated current spending” to: all current spending; all lawful spending; all budgeted spending; all appropriated spending; all historical spending; all governmental programs; all existing governmental entities; or all existing revenue claims.

## 2.2 TRR — Transitional Revenue Requirement

**ESTABLISHED** as a controlled Bill A Definition term (Human Part 2).

TRR is the controlled revenue requirement necessary during the Bill A transition to satisfy **legally obligated current spending** that receives transitional protection under the Human rule.

```text
TRR is temporary.
TRR exists for transition continuity.
TRR SHALL NOT automatically become ARR.
TRR SHALL NOT automatically preserve a current governmental function permanently.
TRR SHALL NOT automatically preserve a current revenue stream.
TRR SHALL NOT automatically preserve a current governmental entity.
```

TRR **SHALL NOT** be calculated in this CWC. Precise legal-obligation classification remains subject to controlled research and later engineering (Q-BILL-A-008 / UQ-TR-014).

TRR is **not** inferred to apply to ALL-OUT. Q-BILL-A-006 established ALL-OUT self-reliance / no Bill A replacement-revenue guarantee. A legal obligation that creates a separate statewide transition problem is a **legal research issue**, not an ALL-OUT revenue guarantee.

## 2.3 ARR — Authorized Revenue Requirement

**ESTABLISHED** as a **separate** controlled Bill A Definition term. **RESERVED** for the mature Future-State revenue requirement after applicable Bill A controls determine what governmental functions, obligations, and spending remain authorized.

Mature Future-State authorization must consider, at minimum, existing Human Intent: constitutional authority; legal authority; function necessity; stewardship; performance; duplication; NBBF accountability; debt/contract obligations; transition outcomes; and other later controlled requirements.

ARR **SHALL NOT** be calculated in this CWC.

## 2.4 TRR / ARR firewall

```text
TRR ≠ ARR.
TRR = TEMPORARY TRANSITION REQUIREMENT.
ARR = MATURE FUTURE-STATE AUTHORIZED REQUIREMENT.
CURRENT SPENDING ≠ TRR AUTOMATICALLY.
TRR ≠ ARR AUTOMATICALLY.
CURRENT REVENUE ≠ TRR.
CURRENT REVENUE ≠ ARR.
HISTORICAL BUDGET ≠ TRR.
HISTORICAL BUDGET ≠ ARR.
```

## 2.5 NBBF new-spending rule

```text
ALL NEW SPENDING IS IMMEDIATELY SUBJECT TO NBBF.
NEW SPENDING → NBBF REVIEW / CONTROL → ONLY THEN MAY AUTHORIZED FUNDING CONSEQUENCES FOLLOW.
```

The transition **SHALL NOT** operate as an open window in which government can create new spending and automatically add it to TRR.

This CWC does **not** invent the complete NBBF admission procedure. It does **not** define every NBBF node requirement. It does **not** silently import noncanonical NBBF mechanics.

## 2.6 Conceptual sequence (not statute)

```text
LEGACY OBLIGATION → TRR PROTECTION WHERE LEGALLY OBLIGATED
NEW SPENDING → NBBF IMMEDIATELY
MATURE SURVIVING GOVERNMENT FUNCTION → NBBF / BILL A AUTHORIZATION CONTROLS → ARR
```

## 2.7 Three-layer architecture (now named)

| Layer | Name | Protected / gated scope |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CURRENT STATE | Existing spending, claims, functions, obligations, entities, debt, contracts, operations. Evidence ≠ entitlement. |
| 2 | TRANSITION (TRR) | Legally obligated current spending as later classified. New spending immediately NBBF. Property tax dissipates toward zero. Revenue above projection accelerates elimination. |
| 3 | MATURE FUTURE STATE (ARR) | Only spending/functions remaining authorized under mature Bill A / NBBF architecture. |

Do **not** calculate Layer 2 or Layer 3.

# 9. Dependency analysis — why Q-BILL-A-008 is next

Q-BILL-A-007 established the **architecture** (TRR vs ARR; legally obligated vs new/NBBF) and left the **admission boundary** undefined.

Two new holes:

1. WHAT COUNTS AS LEGALLY OBLIGATED CURRENT SPENDING (positive TRR membership).  
2. WHAT COUNTS AS NEW SPENDING (NBBF gate / TRR exclusion).

Selecting only (1) would leave a leak: government could create a new contract during transition and claim TRR protection as a “legal obligation.” Selecting only (2) would leave TRR without a positive membership rule.

**Highest-dependency Human decision:** the **single admission boundary** that assigns an ALL-IN transition expenditure to TRR protection or to immediate NBBF. The two classifications are inseparable halves of one policy decision.

That boundary controls: TRR membership; projection for surplus→PT acceleration; sufficiency; no-double-dip; function/obligation audit; whether expansions enter TRR; ALL-IN continuity; later legal research scope; Sedgwick Future-State mapping; later SPEC.

Not selected: senior/disabled eligibility; surplus-after-zero; sinking-fund placement; overlap map; Domain 07; any calculation; inventing NBBF procedure.
