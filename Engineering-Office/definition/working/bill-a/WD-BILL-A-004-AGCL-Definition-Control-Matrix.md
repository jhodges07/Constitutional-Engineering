## 3. Kansas authority firewall

If an AGCL requirement appears incompatible with existing Kansas authority, the conflict SHALL be surfaced for Human Engineer disposition and later constitutional engineering if required.

Q-BILL-A-001 through Q-BILL-A-005 record compatible **intent** with several AGCL controls. Compatible intent is **not** AGCL satisfaction and **not** Kansas legal authority. No control is marked SATISFIED.

Surfaced candidate tensions after Q-BILL-A-005 Option (a):

- closed **compulsory** KLRS vs existing Kansas constitutional tax structure and local home-rule (00A/00H);
- criminal-enforcement intent limited to unauthorized **demands**, not mere receipt (00I) — **not converted to a statute**;
- mixed compulsory/voluntary and monopoly service charges (00A/00C; classification UNCERTAIN until evidence);
- debt/bond/grant receipts in Universe but outside KLRS (00E);
- completeness protocol defined, not certified (00F);
- anti-evasion implementation (00J) LEGAL ENGINEERING REQUIRED;
- motor fuel remains **example, not RETAIN** (CF-BILL-A-006).

Absence of a recorded conflict is **not** compatibility. No control is marked satisfied.
