**Classification:** ENGINEERING INTERPRETATION of selection B.

For ALL-IN transition, TRR covers **only** presently enforceable legal obligations of classes later established through controlled evidence and legal research.

Ongoing budgeted or statutory programs **without** a qualifying enforceable legal instrument are **not** automatically TRR-protected.

New legal instruments created during transition constitute **new spending** subject to NBBF rather than automatically entering TRR.

This interpretation does **not** invent the final qualifying classes. Constitutional obligations, bonded debt, lawful contracts, judgments, and other presently enforceable legal obligations remain **RESEARCH CANDIDATES only** (UQ-TR-016).

# 4. Enforceable-Instrument Class rule

For an ALL-IN county transition, an expenditure may receive TRR protection **only** when it arises from a presently enforceable legal obligation belonging to a qualifying class established through later controlled evidence and legal research.

The two-part test:

1. Is there a presently enforceable legal obligation?  
2. Does it belong to a qualifying class established through controlled evidence/legal research?

Only then may it become **TRR-eligible**.

Mere governmental desire, practice, policy, appropriation, budget inclusion, program continuation, staffing pattern, historical expenditure, historical revenue stream, or administrative expectation is **insufficient by itself**.

The legal obligation must be **real** and **presently enforceable**.

Final qualifying classes are **not invented** in this CWC.

# 6. New-instrument anti-leakage rule

Government **SHALL NOT** create a new legal instrument during transition and thereby automatically transform new spending into a TRR-protected legacy obligation.

```text
NEW CONTRACT → NOT TRR AUTOMATICALLY.
NEW DEBT → NOT TRR AUTOMATICALLY.
NEW OBLIGATION → NOT TRR AUTOMATICALLY.
NEW COMMITMENT → NOT TRR AUTOMATICALLY.
NEW TRANSITION INSTRUMENT → NEW SPENDING → NBBF.
```

Kansas/Bill A NBBF admission procedure remains **UQ-NBBF-001**. **Not invented.**

# 7. Pre-existing instrument status

Every instrument existing before transition does **not** automatically qualify.

```text
PRE-EXISTING ≠ TRR AUTOMATICALLY.
```

The two-part test in §4 still governs.

# 8. TRR temporary character

TRR is Layer 2 — TRANSITION. ARR is Layer 3 — MATURE FUTURE STATE.

An obligation's TRR eligibility during transition **SHALL NOT** automatically:

- preserve the underlying government program;
- preserve the government entity;
- preserve the revenue stream;
- preserve the tax, fee, or assessment;
- establish ARR;
- establish permanent funding;
- establish permanent constitutional authority.

TRR is temporary transitional treatment of qualifying enforceable obligations.
