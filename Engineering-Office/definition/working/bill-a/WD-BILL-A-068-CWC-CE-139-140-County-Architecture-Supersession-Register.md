## 1. Superseded interpretation (exact)

The following understanding is **SUPERSEDED** as controlling Human Engineering Intent:

1. Property-tax elimination is optional by county.  
2. ALL-IN means eliminating property tax.  
3. ALL-OUT means retaining property tax (or non-entry into property-tax elimination).  
4. ALL-IN / ALL-OUT is merely a property-tax-transition participation decision.  
5. A county may permanently remain under the existing system **including property taxation**.

Canonical historical statement of that interpretation (WD-BILL-A-057 §4):

```text
ALL-IN / ALL-OUT is defined during this Definition cycle as a candidate:
PROPERTY-TAX TRANSITION BOUNDARY
not:
GENERAL BILL A PARTICIPATION BOUNDARY.
```

Canonical historical ALL-IN / ALL-OUT meanings (WD-BILL-A-057 §7 / §8; LOU-004 Draft 1.6 §2.8):

```text
ALL-IN (candidate): county affirmatively enters the complete property-tax elimination transition
toward ZERO PROPERTY-TAX AUTHORITY.

ALL-OUT (candidate): the county does not enter that property-tax transition package
at that decision point.
```

Canonical CWC-CE-141 carry-forward (WD-BILL-A-060):

```text
COUNTY ALL-IN / ALL-OUT REMAINS PROPERTY-TAX-TRANSITION-ONLY
CWC-CE-139 / CWC-CE-140: county ALL-IN / ALL-OUT applies only to property-tax transition.
```
