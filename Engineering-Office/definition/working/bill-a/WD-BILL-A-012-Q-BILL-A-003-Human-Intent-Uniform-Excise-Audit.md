## 1. Audit chain (intent)

```text
EXISTING KANSAS EXCISE / EXCISE-TYPE CLAIM
→ AUDIT
→ IDENTIFY LEGAL AUTHORITY
→ IDENTIFY TAXABLE EVENT
→ IDENTIFY CURRENT RATE / CALCULATION
→ IDENTIFY CURRENT STATED PURPOSE
→ TRACE CURRENT REVENUE DESTINATION
→ IDENTIFY ADMINISTRATIVE / OVERHEAD DEDUCTIONS
→ IDENTIFY DEBT / BOND / CONTRACTUAL DEPENDENCIES
→ TEST AGAINST H.R. 25 KANSAS MIRROR
→ TEST AGAINST AGCL CONTROLS
→ HUMAN DISPOSITION DECISION
```

## 2. Fourteen audit questions (each claim)

1. What is being taxed?  
2. What event triggers the claim?  
3. Who legally pays the tax?  
4. What statute or constitutional authority creates it?  
5. What is the current rate or calculation?  
6. What purpose is the tax represented as serving?  
7. Where does every category of revenue currently go?  
8. Does any portion enter general revenue?  
9. Does any portion pay administration, management, accounting, supervision, allocation, distribution, or other overhead?  
10. Does the revenue support bonds, debt, contracts, federal matching requirements, or other existing obligations?  
11. Does the tax overlap or stack with a transaction that would otherwise fall within the Kansas H.R. 25 FairTax mirror?  
12. What would be affected if the claim disappeared?  
13. Does retaining it comply with the applicable AGCL controls?  
14. What unresolved constitutional or statutory issues require primary legal research?  

Answers require PRIMARY-LEGAL / GOV-DATA. AI synthesis is **not** evidence of current Kansas law, rates, collections, statutory purposes, fund destinations, or obligations.

## 3. Uniform surviving-excise standard (if later Human-authorized to survive)

Applies equally to every excise ultimately authorized to survive. Motor fuel is an **example**, not a unique class.

| ID | Rule |
|---|---|
| A | EXPRESS PURPOSE — expressly declared, bounded purpose |
| B | RESTRICTED POOL — 100% restricted to that purpose; not general revenue |
| C | DIRECT PURPOSE EXECUTION — spend only on direct execution of the declared purpose |
| D | NO GOVERNMENT ADMINISTRATIVE OVERHEAD — pool shall not pay admin/managerial/supervisory/accounting/allocation/distribution/general payroll or similar overhead merely because government administers the tax or program. Administration funded outside the pool through otherwise authorized operating resources |
| E | DIRECT LABOR AND EQUIPMENT — only when they directly execute the declared purpose. Motor-fuel/roads example: construction, repair, qualifying maintenance, expressly authorized direct snow-removal (operators, equipment, fuel, salt, materials) may be candidates. Personnel who manage, supervise, account, allocate, administer, or distribute the pool do not qualify merely by administrative relation. DIRECT vs ADMIN test **not yet engineered** |
| F | NO FAIRTAX STACKING — retained excise **or** Kansas FairTax on the same transaction, not automatically both, unless Human later expressly authorizes stacking |
| G | BUSINESS TREATMENT — surviving excise applies per uniform Bill A excise rule; H.R. 25 business-purpose treatment does **not** by itself exempt the excise |
| H | TRANSPARENCY — taxpayer signal: WHAT IS TAXED → RATE → PURPOSE → AMOUNT COLLECTED → RESTRICTED POOL → DIRECT EXPENDITURES → BALANCE → PERFORMANCE / PURPOSE STATUS |
| I | NO AUTOMATIC GRANDFATHERING — existing Kansas law is current-state evidence, not a preservation mandate. Dispositions: RETAIN / TRANSFORM / DISAPPEAR / HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED / EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT |
| J | SILENCE IS NOT AUTHORIZATION — unidentified, unaudited, or unanswered claims are not authorized to survive |
