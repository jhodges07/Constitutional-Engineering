# 7. Overlapping-jurisdiction examples (not universal)

**Example A — City of Wichita, USD 259, tax unit 6702 (2025 mill sheet):**

```text
STATE (1.500)
+ COUNTY (included in “State and County” 29.067)
+ CITY OF WICHITA (32.340)
+ USD 259 (51.402)
= TOTAL LEVY 112.809 mills
```

No township mill in this city tax unit. Fire district mill **not** in this total (29.067 vs 45.821 in fire-district city examples).

**Example B — Unincorporated / fire-district cities:** State + County + Fire District + City (if any) + USD + township and/or specials. Combination **varies**. Derby example mill sheet: TOTAL LEVY 140.604 including USD 260 & Rec Comm.

**Do not assume** every Sedgwick taxpayer is in Wichita + USD 259.

**Example C — Sales/use overlay (Wichita general location, not CID):**

```text
STATE 6.5% + COUNTYWIDE 1.0% + CITY 0.0% = 7.5% combined
```

CID/TDD locations are **higher**. Food: state 0% from 1 Jan 2025; **county 1% still applies** (LegalClarity/KDOR architecture; county 1% not repealed by food phaseout). **LEGAL RESEARCH REQUIRED** for food + CID stacking details.
