# Q-BILL-A-004 DISPOSITION (EXACT HUMAN RECORD)

## 1. Original practical question (preserved; not complete)

The Human Engineer initially wanted to determine whether people in Kansas currently pay excise taxes beyond gasoline / motor-fuel taxes.

That question **remains required** and SHALL be answered by PRIMARY-LEGAL / GOV-DATA evidence during the comprehensive audit.

It is **not** the complete audit objective.

Do not answer it from AI memory.

## 2. Complete revenue-system objective

The Human Engineer wants to know how the entire Kansas governmental revenue system will operate after Bill A becomes law.

Bill A therefore requires an evidenced inventory of all material governmental revenue claims **before** the final post-Bill-A revenue architecture can be defined.

## 3. Kansas Legal Revenue Scope (working Definition concept)

```text
KANSAS LEGAL REVENUE SCOPE
=
the complete, evidenced, closed universe of governmental revenue claims
that Kansas state government and Kansas local governmental entities
may lawfully impose after Bill A's final architecture is established.
```

The intended final scope answers:

**WHAT EXACTLY MAY GOVERNMENT LAWFULLY REQUIRE A KANSAN TO PAY AFTER BILL A?**

This is Human Engineering Intent. It is **not** current Kansas law. The final closed set is **not** populated by this CWC.

## 4. Audit by function — not label

The audit SHALL NOT depend solely upon terminology used by government.

A revenue claim SHALL NOT escape examination merely because it is called something other than a “tax.”

The comprehensive audit shall search for and classify governmental revenue streams including, **where supported by evidence**:

- property taxes;
- income taxes;
- sales taxes;
- use taxes;
- excise taxes;
- privilege taxes;
- severance taxes;
- local-option taxes;
- fees;
- assessments;
- special assessments;
- licenses;
- permits;
- surcharges;
- gallonage charges;
- pack/unit/volume levies;
- compulsory governmental charges;
- governmental service charges;
- penalties or similar receipts where they function materially as revenue mechanisms;
- and other compulsory or materially relevant governmental revenue streams discovered through PRIMARY-LEGAL / GOV-DATA evidence.

**This list is an audit search surface.** It SHALL NOT be treated as a finding that every listed class presently exists, is legally a tax, or will be retained after Bill A.

## 5. State and local scope

The audit requirement SHALL cover, subject to later evidence and jurisdictional classification:

- STATE OF KANSAS; and
- Kansas governmental subdivisions/entities with revenue-claim authority, including classes **discovered through primary evidence** such as: COUNTIES; CITIES; SCHOOL DISTRICTS; TOWNSHIPS; SPECIAL DISTRICTS; AUTHORITIES; and other Kansas governmental entities possessing material revenue-claim authority.

Do **not** invent the complete entity universe. The audit must discover and evidence it.

## 6. Current-state inventory vs post-Bill-A Legal Revenue Scope

Maintain a strict distinction:

```text
CURRENT KANSAS REVENUE INVENTORY
= What Kansas governmental entities are presently authorized to demand,
  assess, collect, receive, or retain under current law.

POST-BILL-A KANSAS LEGAL REVENUE SCOPE
= The Human-controlled closed set of revenue mechanisms Bill A ultimately authorizes.
```

Current existence SHALL NOT create a presumption of survival. No automatic grandfathering.

## 7. Disposition system

Every material revenue claim discovered through the audit shall ultimately require a Human-controlled disposition.

Candidate disposition states remain:

- RETAIN
- TRANSFORM
- DISAPPEAR
- HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED
- EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT

No disposition SHALL be invented under this recording. A blank disposition SHALL NOT mean RETAIN.

## 8. Excise audit relationship (Q-BILL-A-003 preserved)

Q-BILL-A-003 remains controlling Human Intent for the treatment of excise taxes (WD-BILL-A-012). WD-BILL-A-013 remains the excise-domain audit requirement.

The excise audit becomes a **subordinate audit domain** within the Kansas Legal Revenue Scope (WD-BILL-A-016).

All existing Kansas excises and materially equivalent excise-type claims require evidence before disposition.

Any excise later Human-authorized to survive remains subject to the uniform structural standard in WD-BILL-A-012.

Motor fuel remains an **example** of that standard. Motor fuel is **NOT presently RETAINED**.

## 9. Original excise question (must be answered by evidence)

The future audit SHALL specifically answer:

**DO KANSANS CURRENTLY PAY EXCISE TAXES OR MATERIALLY EQUIVALENT EXCISE-TYPE CLAIMS BEYOND MOTOR-FUEL / GASOLINE TAXATION?**

The answer must be derived from PRIMARY-LEGAL / GOV-DATA. Do not answer from AI memory. This CWC does **not** answer it.

## 10. Closed revenue authority (intent, not current law)

POST-BILL-A GOVERNMENT REVENUE AUTHORITY SHALL BE CLOSED RATHER THAN OPEN-ENDED.

Candidate working rule:

```text
IF A GOVERNMENTAL REVENUE CLAIM IS NOT WITHIN THE FINAL
KANSAS LEGAL REVENUE SCOPE, GOVERNMENT HAS NO AUTHORITY
UNDER THE BILL A ARCHITECTURE TO DEMAND IT.
```

This is Human Engineering Intent. It is **NOT** a claim about current Kansas law.

## 11. No revenue authority by silence

No Kansas state or local governmental entity may obtain revenue authority merely because:

- the Human Engineer did not identify the claim;
- the audit missed the claim;
- the claim existed historically;
- the mechanism is renamed;
- the mechanism is characterized as a fee rather than a tax;
- the mechanism is imposed locally rather than statewide;
- or the mechanism performs substantially the same economic function under different terminology.

The precise legal implementation of this anti-evasion principle remains **LEGAL ENGINEERING REQUIRED**.

## 12. Human criminal-enforcement intent (not current law; not legislation)

Record separately from current-law findings:

The Human Engineer intends Bill A ultimately to prohibit Kansas state government, Kansas local government, and persons exercising governmental revenue authority from knowingly imposing, assessing, collecting, or compelling payment of a governmental revenue claim outside the final Kansas Legal Revenue Scope.

The Human Engineer intends unauthorized governmental revenue demands outside that closed scope to be subject to criminal enforcement.

**THIS IS HUMAN ENGINEERING INTENT.**

This CWC does **not** convert this statement into operative criminal legislation, define a criminal offense as current law, or establish criminal penalties.
