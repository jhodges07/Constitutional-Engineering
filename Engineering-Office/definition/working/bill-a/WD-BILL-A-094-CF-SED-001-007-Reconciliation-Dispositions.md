# 2. CF-SED-001 — County ACFR property taxes vs PVD county levy

**Items**

| Source | Exact / reported amount | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Sedgwick County ACFR YE 31 Dec 2025, governmental funds **Property taxes** | **$241,512,296** (MD&A **$241.5 million**) | GAAP property-tax **revenue** recognized in County FY 2025. Composition: GF **$187,769,704**; Debt Service **$10,204,888**; Other governmental **$43,537,704**. Special assessments are a **separate** line (**$354,169**) — **not** inside $241.5M. |
| Kansas PVD 2025 Sedgwick card, **County** district class | **$205,980,345.17** | Tax year **2025** ad valorem **levy** for the PVD County class. **Does not** include PVD Fire class **$23,655,788.73**. **Does not** include motor-vehicle in-lieu. |

**Verified accounting/legal facts (not a complete dollar bridge)**

1. County ACFR notes: taxes levied during the current year finance the **ensuing** year’s budget; the Treasurer may not distribute taxes collected in the year levied prior to January 1 of the ensuing year; at December 31 those taxes are receivable with **deferred inflows**. Deferred property tax revenue **$224,514,183** is recorded. Therefore PVD **tax year 2025** county levy is **not** the recognition-year match for ACFR FY2025 property-tax **revenue**.
2. Government-wide note: “Property taxes are recognized as revenues in the year **for which they are levied**.” Fund note: property taxes “are budgeted to finance the **subsequent year’s** operations and, consequently, are not susceptible to accrual.” Those sentences describe Kansas levy/budget timing; they do **not** make $241.5M equal $206.0M.
3. Motor-vehicle in-lieu is **observed** at County Q4 GF **$18,146,020**. The governmental-funds revenue statement has **no** separate motor-vehicle line; MV is a **candidate** component of “Property taxes” or is otherwise classified. **NOT DETERMINED** as a complete MV inclusion proof.
4. Fire District Operating is a County ACFR special-revenue schedule. PVD **Fire** is a **separate** class from PVD **County**. If Fire District levy is inside ACFR “Property taxes,” that is a **scope** difference from the PVD County line. **PARTIALLY VERIFIED as a class**; dollar assignment **NOT DETERMINED**.
5. Special assessments **$354,169** are **outside** the $241.5M property-tax total.

**Disposition:** these are **unlike quantities** (levy year vs recognition year; levy vs GAAP revenue mix; County-class levy vs blended County reporting that may include Fire and in-lieu). A **complete authoritative line-item bridge** (treasurer collection report by component equaling $241,512,296) was **not** constructed.

**CF-SED-001 REMAINS UNRESOLVED as a numerical bridge.** Do not manufacture $241.5M = $206.0M + motor vehicle + Fire + delinquencies without that report.

# 8. CF-SED-007 — KU collection-derived taxable-sales vs food-phaseout rates

This CWC does **not** attack or endorse KU generally. It classifies the method against KDOR primary files.

**Verified KDOR structure:** `cy24revised.xlsx` and `cy23revised.xlsx` split **General** (6.5%) and **Food** (then-effective food rate) and publish **Combined** collections **and** a **Taxable Sales** column.

CY2024 Sedgwick Combined **Taxable Sales** column: **$11,136,517,444.15**. KU Abstract Sedgwick 2024 nominal taxable sales **$11,136.5 million** matches that KDOR Combined Taxable Sales figure (rounding). That is **not** Combined collections **$654,923,412.48** divided by 6.5%.

CY2025: no annual revised workbook. KU 2025 Sedgwick **$9,996.5 million** is arithmetically consistent with dividing the verified CY2025 **state sales** collections **$649,772,028.02** by 6.5%. **This CWC does not adopt that quotient as a tax base.** During 2025, state food rate is **0%**, so food sales generate **$0** state collections and **disappear** from a collections÷6.5% “taxable sales” measure. That is a **material distortion** if the measure is used as a continuation of 2024 Combined Taxable Sales (which included food converted at 2%).

**Limitation classified:** dividing remaining state collections by the general 6.5% rate **does not** produce a uniform taxable-sales estimate across categories or years when food (and any other non-6.5% category) is taxed at a different state rate or at 0%. Use KU / Taxable Sales columns as **discovery only**. **Not** a Bill A base.

**Disposition: CLOSED as methodological classification.**
