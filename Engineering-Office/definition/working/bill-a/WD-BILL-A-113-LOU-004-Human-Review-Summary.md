## 1. What Bill A is intended to accomplish

Replace the Kansas tax architecture so that **property rights precede government pooling**, **signal precedes claim**, and there is **one ultimate source of taxation: people with money**. Shuffling collectors, entities, or tax names does not create another source. Current tax revenue is not automatically required replacement revenue. Current spending is not automatically authorized future spending. Taxpayers are not automatic backstops for government planning or performance failures.

## 2. State tax architecture

Kansas **state** legacy tax architecture converts to the selected **H.R. 25-style** consumption-tax architecture (119th Congress IH economic standard, Kansas-adapted without silently changing that standard). The state does **not** keep a parallel traditional state income/payroll system merely because one exists today. Existence, ownership, possession, accumulation, and appreciation are **not** the H.R. 25-style taxable event. Rates are **not set**.

## 3. Property-tax elimination

**PROPERTY TAX → ZERO STATEWIDE.** Every Kansas county eliminates property taxation. ALL-IN / ALL-OUT does **not** decide whether property tax survives. Each county chooses a **5-year or 7-year maximum runway**, not a guaranteed collection period. ALL-IN sufficiency may accelerate elimination. Qualifying seniors and qualifying disabled persons on fixed incomes have first priority to zero, then other taxpayers. Eligibility mechanics remain later.

## 4. ALL-IN

Participates in the new Bill A state/local architecture. Current State → **TRR** → **ARR**. New spending → **NBBF**. Surplus above the controlled transition requirement/projection accelerates remaining property-tax elimination. No double dip. Authorized compulsory claims follow Bill A controls including GCD / Zero Terminal where applicable.

## 5. ALL-OUT

Does **not** participate in the new **local** H.R. 25-style replacement. Receives **no** replacement-revenue guarantee. Still eliminates property tax. Still subject to statewide taxpayer-control architecture. May operate only claims Bill A authorizes to survive. May operate bounded traditional personal/business income/payroll where authorized. **Shall not** impose inheritance tax. May not revive eliminated claims. Income/payroll follows GCD to Zero Terminal. ALL-OUT is **not** a permanent legacy tax system and **not** a permanent tax floor.

## 6. TRR

**Transitional Revenue Requirement** (Layer 2). Q-BILL-A-008 = **B — Enforceable-Instrument Class**. A TRR candidate must be a **presently enforceable legal obligation** of a later-established qualifying class. Current spending, historical spending, budgets, appropriations, programs, and pre-existing status are **not** automatically TRR. Classes remain research (UQ-TR-016). **Not calculated.**

## 7. ARR

**Authorized Revenue Requirement** (Layer 3, mature Future State). **TRR ≠ ARR.** TRR does not automatically become ARR. **Not calculated.**

## 8. NBBF role

All **new spending** is immediately subject to NBBF, including new transition instruments. Kansas/Bill A NBBF admission procedure is **not invented** (UQ-NBBF-001). Four-month node accountability remains high-level Human Intent; mechanics are not invented.

## 9. Government Claim Degradation

Authorized compulsory governmental monetary claims subject to GCD must contain and follow an authorized degradation path. **Substance controls over label.** Degradation does **not** authorize a claim. Authorization must come first.

## 10. Zero Terminal

Q-BILL-A-009 = **A**. General destination is **ZERO**. No automatic non-zero floor. Percentage path: **−0.25 percentage points every three months** (not 25%, not proportional, not compounded). Annual liability uses the **arithmetic average** of applicable rates (example 9.625%). Fixed-dollar path: **1% of original starting amount per year**, not compounded.

## 11. Sinking-fund objective

**Personal sinking funds ≠ governmental sinking funds.** Future-State objective: accumulated capital and authorized earnings may increasingly support authorized functions as compulsory claims degrade toward zero. **Not** proven financial sufficiency. Returns are **not** assumed.

## 12. Taxpayer property-right principle

Property rights precede pooling. Signal must precede claim. GCD and governmental sinking funds shall **not** silently convert taxpayer property into permanent government ownership. Downstream custody/ownership mechanics remain unresolved.
