## 3. 16M / 20M classification

**Shorthand meaning (from PRIMARY-LEGAL, not county guides):** motor vehicles with **gross vehicle weight more than 12,000 pounds but less than 20,001 pounds**.

**Authoritative citation:** K.S.A. **79-5105a** (2025 K.S.A. PDF). History: L. 1997, ch. 187, § 5; L. 1998, ch. 140, § 3; January 1, 1999. **CURRENT.**

**Rule:** For tax year 1998 and each year thereafter, personal property tax on those vehicles **shall be computed in accordance with K.S.A. 79-5101 et seq.**, but **shall be paid at the time required by K.S.A. 79-2004a** (personal-property installment dates: Dec. 20 / May 10).

**Why distinct from KRU-D02-012:** 79-5101(c) **excludes** vehicles registered for GVW more than 12,000 pounds from the motor-vehicle tax **act** (registration-time collection). 79-5105a **re-applies the 79-5101 computation** to the 12,001–20,000 lb band but **does not** use registration-time payment. Official PVD 2026 Personal Property Summary confirms 20% assessment / formula value / taxes paid in arrears, citing 79-5105a.

**Classification:** **DISTINCT CURRENT Domain 02 compulsory property claim.** Added **KRU-D02-016**. Disposition **BLANK**. Not Domain 05 (not the 8-143m CMV fee). Not a duplicate of tax-roll 24,000 lb+ FMV/30% vehicles.

County appraiser guides (Barton/Riley/Miami) assisted discovery only.
