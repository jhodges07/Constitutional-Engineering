The Human Engineer intends Bill A's architecture to **investigate** a county-by-county property-tax transition mechanism.

Contemplated structure (intent only; architecture remains under Engineering Definition):

```text
KANSAS STATEWIDE BILL A ARCHITECTURE
→ STATEWIDE API / DATA ACCESS
→ STATEWIDE TAXPAYER SUPREMACY
```

# 10. NO-CHERRY-PICKING PRINCIPLE

Human Engineering Intent: an ALL-IN county should not be permitted to selectively retain pieces of the legacy property-tax architecture while simultaneously claiming selected benefits of the property-tax replacement architecture.

Candidate principles:

```text
ALL-IN MEANS COMPLETE ENTRY INTO THE DEFINED PROPERTY-TAX TRANSITION PACKAGE.

ALL-OUT MEANS THE COUNTY DOES NOT ENTER THAT PROPERTY-TAX TRANSITION PACKAGE AT THAT DECISION POINT.
```

This principle applies to the **PROPERTY-TAX TRANSITION PACKAGE**.

It SHALL NOT be broadened to mean that an ALL-OUT county escapes statewide Bill A architecture.

The exact contents of the property-tax transition package remain subject to later Engineering Definition. This CWC does not inventory that package as a closed list.
