# Q-BILL-A-005 — HISTORICAL ASKED WORDING (2026-09-02)

For the **post-Bill-A Kansas Legal Revenue Scope** (the closed set of revenue mechanisms government may lawfully require a Kansan to pay, or otherwise demand, after Bill A): shall that closed set include (a) **only compulsory governmental claims** (taxes, compulsory fees, assessments, licenses/permits where payment is legally required, and other legally compelled charges), with other governmental receipts inventoried in the current-state register but **outside** the closed demand-authority set unless later expressly added; (b) **compulsory claims plus** expressly listed non-compulsory receipt classes you name (for example grants, donations, federal funds, investment earnings, asset sales, borrowing/bond proceeds, enterprise/utility charges, court fines, tuition, or others you specify); or (c) **all material governmental receipts** discovered in the current-state audit, unless later expressly excluded? You are not asked to RETAIN / TRANSFORM / DISAPPEAR any current Kansas claim. The current-state audit may still **discover and classify** all of the above pending this answer.

# Q-BILL-A-005 DISPOSITION (EXACT HUMAN RECORD)

**OPTION (a).**

The post-Bill-A Kansas Legal Revenue Scope shall be the closed set of **compulsory governmental revenue claims** that government may lawfully demand from a person or organization.

The comprehensive current-state audit shall nevertheless discover and classify **all material governmental receipts**, including non-compulsory receipts, but those receipts shall be maintained **outside** the closed demand-authority set unless the Human Engineer later expressly determines otherwise.

Receipt of money by government does **not** itself create authority to compel payment from a Kansan.

The audit must preserve the distinction between:

```text
GOVERNMENT REVENUE RECEIVED
≠
GOVERNMENT PAYMENT DEMANDED UNDER GOVERNMENTAL AUTHORITY
```

This is Human Engineering Intent. It is **not** current Kansas law. It does **not** RETAIN any existing revenue claim. It does **not** authorize a new revenue claim. It does **not** establish a criminal offense.

## 1. Core Definition (working)

```text
KANSAS GOVERNMENT REVENUE UNIVERSE
= The complete evidenced universe of material monetary inflows, receipts,
  compulsory claims, transfers, financing proceeds, and other material
  governmental revenue/receipt classes discovered for Kansas state and
  local governmental entities.

KANSAS LEGAL REVENUE SCOPE
= The intended closed post-Bill-A set of COMPULSORY GOVERNMENTAL
  REVENUE CLAIMS that government is affirmatively authorized to impose,
  assess, collect, or otherwise compel from persons or organizations.
```

Therefore:

```text
ALL KLRS CLAIMS ARE WITHIN THE GOVERNMENT REVENUE UNIVERSE.
NOT ALL GOVERNMENT RECEIPTS ARE KLRS CLAIMS.
```

Uncertain classifications remain UNCERTAIN / EVIDENCE REQUIRED. Do not silently resolve them.

## 2. Candidate system model

```text
KANSAS GOVERNMENT REVENUE UNIVERSE
→ CLASSIFY RECEIPT / CLAIM
→ COMPULSORY GOVERNMENT DEMAND?
    YES
    → KANSAS LEGAL REVENUE SCOPE ANALYSIS
    → CURRENT AUTHORITY
    → BILL A DISPOSITION
    → FINAL CLOSED DEMAND-AUTHORITY SET

    NO / NOT ESTABLISHED
    → OTHER GOVERNMENT RECEIPT CLASS
    → INVENTORY / TRACE / CONTROL
    → NOT AUTOMATICALLY GOVERNMENT DEMAND AUTHORITY
```

Discovery-surface examples (not findings; not compulsory/non-compulsory declarations): federal transfers; intergovernmental transfers; grants; donations; investment earnings; asset sales; borrowing; bond proceeds; enterprise receipts; voluntary service payments; fines; penalties; forfeitures; settlements; restitution; unclaimed property; and other material receipts discovered through evidence.

## 3. Closed demand-authority principle (intent, not current law)

POST-BILL-A GOVERNMENTAL DEMAND AUTHORITY IS CLOSED.

Candidate working test:

```text
GOVERNMENT SEEKS PAYMENT
→ IDENTIFY CLAIM
→ IDENTIFY GOVERNMENT ENTITY
→ IDENTIFY LEGAL AUTHORITY
→ IDENTIFY TRIGGER / TAXABLE EVENT
→ TEST FINAL KANSAS LEGAL REVENUE SCOPE
→ AUTHORIZED OR UNAUTHORIZED.
```

If outside the final Kansas Legal Revenue Scope: **NO BILL-A REVENUE-COLLECTION AUTHORITY.**

Later legal consequences remain LEGAL / LEGISLATIVE ENGINEERING REQUIRED. This working Definition is **not** operative law.

## 4. Anti-evasion (audit/classification principle)

A compulsory demand shall not escape audit merely because it is labeled fee, assessment, license, permit, surcharge, service charge, special assessment, privilege charge, or another term.

Candidate classification SHALL consider: LEGAL LABEL + LEGAL AUTHORITY + PAYMENT TRIGGER + WHO MUST PAY + CONSEQUENCE OF NONPAYMENT + ECONOMIC FUNCTION + REVENUE DESTINATION.

This is **not** a current-law conclusion.
