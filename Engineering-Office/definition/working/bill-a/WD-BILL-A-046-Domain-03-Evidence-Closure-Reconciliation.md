## 3. CF-D03-002 — Privilege tax / corporate income exclusion

**PRIMARY-LEGAL (retrieved 2026-09-02):** K.S.A. **79-32,113(c)**:

> In addition to the persons or organizations exempt from federal income taxation under the provision of the federal internal revenue code, there shall also be exempt from the tax imposed by this act, **insurance companies, banks, trust companies, savings and loan associations, credit unions** and any other organizations, entities or persons specifically exempt from Kansas income taxation under the laws of the state of Kansas.

**79-1106** remains: privilege tax **in lieu of ad valorem taxes** that might otherwise be imposed upon the **intangible assets** of the listed institutions — not an “in lieu of corporate income tax” clause.

**79-1107(b)** (banks): the levied privilege tax shall be in lieu of ad valorem taxes on shares of capital stock or intangible assets.

**Classification:** **RESOLVED — DIFFERENT LEGAL CLAIMS.**

| Institution class | Ordinary corporate income tax (79-32,110(c)) | Privilege tax (79-1106 et seq.) |
|---|---|---|
| National banking associations / state banks | Exempt (79-32,113(c) “banks”) | Taxed (79-1107) |
| Trust companies | Exempt (79-32,113(c)) | Taxed (79-1108) |
| Savings and loan associations | Exempt (79-32,113(c)) | Taxed (79-1108) |
| Federally chartered savings banks | Treated as privilege taxpayers by 79-1106; 79-32,113(c) names “savings and loan associations” rather than this phrase | **LEGAL INTERPRETATION REQUIRED** as to verbatim subclass naming; KDOR K-130 practice treats them as privilege filers (implementation evidence, not a third claim) |
| Credit unions | Exempt from Income Tax Act (79-32,113(c)) | **Not** listed in 79-1106. **Not** added as a Domain 03 privilege row |
| Insurance companies | Exempt from Income Tax Act (79-32,113(c)) | Premium tax — **not Domain 03** (already referred) |
| Ordinary corporations | Taxed (79-32,110(c)) | Not privilege taxpayers |

KDOR 2025 Corporate booklet restates the 79-32,113(c) exemption and prohibits consolidating an income-tax taxpayer with a privilege-tax taxpayer. That is **implementation evidence** corroborating the statute; the exclusion does **not** rest on instructions alone.

KRU-D03-002 and KRU-D03-003 remain **separate**. Do not double-count fiscal lines.

## 5. PTE fiscal + 79-32,288

**79-32,288 RETRIEVED** (2025 K.S.A. PDF; L. 2022, ch. 63, § 5; July 1):

- Electing PTE owners **shall not be liable** for the tax under the act **in their separate or individual capacities**.
- Owners subject to Kansas individual income tax receive a **credit** equal to their **direct share** of the 79-32,286 tax.
- Excess credit after other credits is **refundable**.
- Credit may not exceed the direct share of PTE tax reported by the entity.
- Basis in the interest/stock is determined **as if the election had not been made**.

**Fiscal:** Isolated statewide **actual** PTE receipts: **EVIDENCE REQUIRED**.

Official treatment (KLRD CRE Long Memo 05-05-2023): SALT Parity is **net neutral for state receipts** but **shifts payments from individual income tax to corporation income tax** and changes timing. FY2023 corporation estimate was increased **$602.0 million** with SALT Parity as a stated driver — that is an **estimate / classification shift**, **not** isolated actual PTE collections. HB 2239 supplemental note: SALT Parity fiscal effect **indeterminate**. KLRD FY2026 monthly SGF receipts discuss “SALT Parity Act taxpayers” as a **monitored subset of corporation receipts**, not a published annual isolated actual.

**Double-count control:** Do **not** add PTE gross entity payments to KRU-D03-001 or KRU-D03-002 as additional net SGF revenue. Owner credit (79-32,288) is the anti-double-tax mechanism. KRU-D03-004 remains a distinct **claim** because the entity is the statutory taxpayer when the election is in force.

## 6. K.S.A. 12-1,101 local implementation

**Official implementation source:** KDOR **Form 200** Local Intangibles Tax Return and rate list.

- 2025 packet: `https://www.ksrevenue.gov/pdf/20025.pdf` — rates “provided by local County Clerks” current to **July 1, 2025**.
- KDOR intangibles page: `https://ksrevenue.gov/perstaxtypesint.html`
- 2026 packet exists (Form 200 Rev. 8-21-25); use KDOR page for the current official file.

Form 200 distinguishes **county rate** (often **0%** or **0.75%**) from **city** and **township** rates (commonly **2.25%**, the statutory city/township cap). If a city/township is not listed, a return may still be required for a **county** portion.

**Examples (Form 200; not a complete inventory):** Cheyenne, Clark, Clay, Cloud, Cowley counties listed at **0.75%**; Atchison, Brown, Decatur and others listed at **0%** county with named cities/townships at **2.25%**.
