## 4. Valuation chain (do not simplify)

```text
PROPERTY
→ CLASSIFICATION (Art. 11 §1 / 79-1439)
→ APPRAISED VALUE
     non-ag real: fair market value (79-503a; 79-1476(b))
     agricultural land: use / productivity (Art. 11 §12; 79-1476)
     public utility: PVD unit FMV as of January 1 (79-5a04)
     CIME personal: retail-cost-new less depreciation, 20% floor
→ ASSESSED VALUE = appraised × subclass %
→ MILL LEVY (79-1801 certification; overlapping districts)
→ TAX LIABILITY
```

Assessment percentages (79-1439 / Art. 11 §1): residential 11.5%; ag land 30% of **use** value; vacant lots 12%; specified 501(c) 12%; public-utility real 33% (railroad at commercial average); commercial/industrial 25%; other real 30%. Personal subclasses as in WD-BILL-A-030 / KRU-D02-001 notes.
