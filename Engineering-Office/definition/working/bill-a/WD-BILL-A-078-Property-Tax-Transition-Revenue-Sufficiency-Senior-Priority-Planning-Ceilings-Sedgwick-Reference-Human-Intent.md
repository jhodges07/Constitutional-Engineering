# 5. NO-DOUBLE-DIP

**HUMAN ENGINEERING INTENT**

```text
STATE AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT SHALL NOT USE THE TRANSITION PERIOD
TO COLLECT FULL REPLACEMENT-SYSTEM REVENUE WHILE ALSO CONTINUING
TO COLLECT PROPERTY-TAX REVENUE THAT IS NO LONGER NECESSARY
UNDER THE CONTROLLED TRANSITION.

THE TRANSITION IS NOT AUTHORITY TO DOUBLE-DIP.
```

The existence of a nominal 5-year or 7-year schedule does **not** independently authorize continued property taxation after the subsequently defined revenue-sufficiency condition has been achieved.

**NO-DOUBLE-DIP ≠ FORMULA YET.**

**HUMAN ENGINEERING INTENT**

```text
QUALIFYING SENIOR HOMESTEAD
→
PROPERTY TAX = ZERO IN YEAR 1
```

subject to later legal, constitutional, fiscal, eligibility, and implementation engineering.

This is an **elimination priority**, not merely:

- a freeze;
- a valuation cap;
- a deferral;
- a rebate;
- a partial exemption;
- a permanent separate property-tax class.

The intended destination is **zero**.

Do **not** define senior first-year elimination as:

```text
YOUNGER PROPERTY OWNERS PAY THE SENIORS' FORMER PROPERTY TAX
```
