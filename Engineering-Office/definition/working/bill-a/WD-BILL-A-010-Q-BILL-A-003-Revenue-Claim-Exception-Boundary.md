## 4. Candidate exception control model

**HUMAN-ACCEPTANCE REQUIRED.** Not an accepted requirement. Not a SPEC. Not legislation.

### 4.1 Default

A Kansas revenue claim **disappears or is transformed** into the Kansas FairTax architecture unless Human Engineering Intent **and** later legal engineering expressly authorize survival.

Existence of a current tax, fee, levy, assessment, surcharge, excise, license charge, or similar mechanism does **not** authorize survival.

### 4.2 Exception

A non-FairTax revenue claim may survive only when **expressly identified**.
