## 6. UNK-D04-005 — CID / TDD / STAR enabling authority

One class row **KRU-D04-005** is preserved. Multiple enabling statutes do **not** by themselves require fragmentation.

### CID — K.S.A. 12-6a31 (2025 PDF retrieved)

- **Additional** RST-base tax in the district, **notwithstanding** 12-187–12-197 aggregate caps.  
- Increments **0.10% or 0.25%**, **not to exceed 2%**.  
- Purpose: finance a project; pledge to **bonds** or pay-as-you-go reimbursement.  
- Duration: expire no later than bond maturity; pay-as-you-go expires **22 years** from first KDOR collection or when costs paid.  
- KDOR collects like state RST; 2% administration credit to CID admin fund, **capped $200,000/FY**; remainder to municipality CID fund.

**Materially distinct additional overlay:** YES, within the KRU-D04-005 class.

### TDD — K.S.A. 12-17,145 (2025 PDF retrieved)

- **Additional** RST-base tax in the transportation development district, notwithstanding 12-187–12-197 caps.  
- Increments **0.10% or 0.25%**, **not to exceed 1%**.  
- Expire when project paid or **no later than bond maturity**.  
- KDOR collects; remitted via transportation development district sales tax fund.

**Materially distinct additional overlay:** YES, same **class** as CID (different cap/purpose), **not** a new KRU row.

### STAR — K.S.A. 12-17,169 (2025 PDF retrieved)

Special-obligation / TIF-style bonds payable from, among other sources, pledges of **tax increment** of **existing** local sales and use taxes collected in the project area and, optionally, **state** sales-tax increment. This is **destination / pledge of KRU-D04-001 / 003 / 004 receipts**, not an automatic additional rate like CID/TDD.

State **2%** redevelopment overlay in 79-3603 / 79-3703 remains a **rate overlay of KRU-D04-001/002**, not a sixth counted claim.

**Inventory decision:** STAR increment **does not** create KRU-D04-006. Documented under KRU-D04-005 notes + 001/002 overlay + 003/004 destination.

| Class | Bond/debt |
|---|---|
| CID / TDD additional tax pledged to project/bonds | **POTENTIAL DEPENDENCY** |
| STAR increment pledge of existing RST/use | **DEPENDENCY VERIFIED** as statutory pledge capacity; **POTENTIAL DEPENDENCY** as to particular projects |
| Impairment if the pledged claim disappeared | **LEGAL EFFECT UNKNOWN** |

Dependency ≠ RETAIN. No impairment analysis. No redevelopment-policy engineering.

## 9. UNK-D04-008 — K.S.A. 12-189a

**Retrieved:** 2025 Kansas Statutes PDF, `012_001_0089a_k.pdf`. Catchline: **Same; certain sales exempt from state sales tax subject to local tax.**

Local taxes under **12-187 et seq.** still apply to:

- (a) mains-delivered residential/agricultural gas, electricity, heat, and water (water subsection expired January 1, 2006);  
- (b) residential propane/LP/coal/wood/other fuel for noncommercial heat/lighting;  
- (c) noncommercial intrastate telephone/telegraph;  
- (d) **food and food ingredients**.

History: L. 1979, ch. 326, § 4; L. 2003, ch. 147, § 29; **L. 2022, ch. 89, § 2; January 1, 2023**. **Current**, not superseded.

This is the **statutory exception** to 12-189’s “identical in application and exemptions to the Kansas retailers’ sales tax act.” It is why **local food tax remains** after the **state** food rate is 0%. **Not a new Domain 04 claim.** Strengthens KRU-D04-003 notes and CF-D04-002.
