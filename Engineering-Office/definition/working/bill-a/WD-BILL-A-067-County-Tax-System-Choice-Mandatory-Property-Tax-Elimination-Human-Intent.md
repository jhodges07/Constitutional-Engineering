# 4. THREE SEPARATE CONCEPTS

## 4.1 Statewide mandatory property-tax elimination

Property taxation is eliminated throughout Kansas.

County participation in property-tax elimination is **NOT** optional.

There is **no** county option to retain property taxation permanently.

The final condition for every Kansas county is:

```text
PROPERTY TAX = ZERO
```

```text
ALL-OUT ≠ RETAIN PROPERTY TAX
ALL-IN ≠ PROPERTY-TAX ELIMINATION
PROPERTY-TAX ELIMINATION OCCURS UNDER BOTH PATHS
ALL-IN / ALL-OUT DOES NOT DETERMINE WHETHER PROPERTY TAX IS ELIMINATED
```

The legal, constitutional, jurisdictional, debt, school-finance, municipal, special-district, and transition **mechanisms** necessary to accomplish that result remain:

**RESEARCH REQUIRED** / **LEGAL INTERPRETATION REQUIRED** / **NOT DETERMINED**

as appropriate. This CWC does **not** invent those mechanisms.

This CWC does **not** establish that the architecture is presently authorized by Kansas law.

## 4.2 County choice — elimination schedule

Each county chooses **one** of two authorized property-tax elimination schedules:

- **A.** Five-year elimination schedule  
- **B.** Seven-year elimination schedule  

The Human Engineer has established the **existence** of these two choices.

This CWC SHALL NOT invent:

- annual reduction percentages;

- mill-levy formulas;
- election mechanics;
- decision authority;
- election timing;

- transition formulas;

Those remain later Definition/research questions (WD-BILL-A-069).

The schedule choice is **independent** of the tax-system choice unless later Human Engineering Intent establishes a dependency.

**CWC-CE-146 INTEGRATION (does not invent mechanics):** the chosen 5-year or 7-year schedule is the **outside maximum authorized runway**, not a governmental entitlement to collect property tax for the full period. Earlier zero is intended when subsequently defined revenue-sufficiency conditions are satisfied (WD-BILL-A-078). Annual reduction mathematics remain UQ-CTS-001 / **NOT INVENTED**.

## 4.3 County choice — tax system

Separately from the five-year / seven-year schedule, each county chooses between:

**ALL-IN**  
Adopt the new Bill A tax system applicable to county/local taxation, subject to later accepted Engineering Definition and Requirements.

**ALL-OUT**  
Retain the otherwise applicable legacy tax system, **EXCEPT** property taxation is permanently removed.

```text
ALL-IN  = NEW SYSTEM
ALL-OUT = LEGACY SYSTEM MINUS PROPERTY TAX
```

Do **not** infer which individual legacy taxes, fees, assessments, licenses, excises, or other governmental claims survive under ALL-OUT. Those dispositions remain **BLANK** / **NOT DETERMINED** unless separately decided by the Human Engineer.
