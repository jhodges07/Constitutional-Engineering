## 2.2 Human Engineering Intent recorded from Q-BILL-A-001 (2026-08-31)

**Classification:** HUMAN INTENT / WORKING DEFINITION.

**Source of record:** `Engineering-Office/definition/working/bill-a/WD-BILL-A-007-Q-BILL-A-001-Human-Intent-Working-Definition.md` (SRC-BILL-A-012).

**Not:** a specification; legislative language; a claim of current Kansas law; HG-D1 acceptance; a maturity change; publication.

The Human Engineer states that the fundamental problem Bill A must solve is that the existing tax architecture can hide, unbound, and invert the proper order of property, signal, claim, pooling, stewardship, and accountability — treating government revenue need as if it created property rights, additional tax sources, unlimited ownership of collected resources, a permanent rate entitlement, and an automatic taxpayer backstop.

Bill A is to be considered structurally successful only if the replacement architecture can establish and protect the objectives listed in WD-BILL-A-007 §14, including at least:

- property rights before government pooling;
- clear signals before revenue claims;
- one ultimate source of taxation — people with money;
- clear separation between tax source and tax collection mechanism;
- visible taxpayer burden;
- explicitly defined taxable events;
- bounded government revenue authority;
- taxpayer ownership as an engineering objective and government stewardship rather than unlimited ownership;
- auditable constitutional spending authority at state and local levels;
- sinking-fund financial discipline;
- non-permanent assumptions about tax rates;
- an end to taxpayers as the automatic backstop for poor planning;
- four-month node accountability with controlled consequences for node failure;
- transparency of collection and expenditure;
- expiration / reauthorization where required;
- enforceable controls against government drift.

Working architectural chain (intent only; architecture remains under Engineering Definition):

```text
PERSON WITH ECONOMIC RESOURCES
→ PROPERTY RIGHT
→ CLEAR GOVERNMENT SIGNAL
→ AUTHORIZED TAXABLE EVENT
→ BOUNDED REVENUE CLAIM
→ COLLECTION / TRANSMISSION MECHANISM
→ GOVERNMENT POOLING
→ TAXPAYER-OWNERSHIP / STEWARDSHIP CONTROL
→ CONSTITUTIONALLY AUTHORIZED SPENDING
→ GOVERNMENT NODE
→ FOUR-MONTH ACCOUNTABILITY
→ PERFORMANCE SIGNAL
→ CONTINUED AUTHORITY / CORRECTIVE ACTION / FAILURE RESPONSE
→ EXPIRATION OR REAUTHORIZATION
```

The Human Engineer expressly reserved legal feasibility of continuing taxpayer ownership after payment, constitutional spending boundaries, tax incidence, legality of taxable events, rates, replacement-revenue quantity, node definition, and node-failure legal consequences as matters requiring research and Human disposition. This LOU records that reservation. It does **not** convert those items into facts.

## 2.3 Human Engineering Intent recorded from Q-BILL-A-002 (2026-09-01)

**Classification:** HUMAN INTENT / WORKING DEFINITION — governing **classification architecture** for end-state tax design.

**Source of record:** `Engineering-Office/definition/working/bill-a/WD-BILL-A-008-Q-BILL-A-002-Human-Intent-FairTax-Architecture.md` (SRC-BILL-A-013).

**Primary federal model:** H.R. 25 — FairTax Act of 2025 (119th Congress, Introduced / IH), pinned for Definition tracing at congress.gov / `BILLS-119hr25ih.pdf` (SRC-BILL-A-015). Crosswalk surface: WD-BILL-A-009 (SRC-BILL-A-014).

**Not:** a specification; legislative language; a claim of current Kansas law; a fiscal finding; HG-D1 or HG-D2 acceptance; a maturity change; publication; commit/push authorization; a completed Kansas tax-class inventory.

Governing disposition rule (intent only):

```text
H.R. 25 ECONOMIC STANDARD
→ KANSAS MIRROR
→ EXPRESSLY IDENTIFIED KANSAS DEVIATIONS ONLY
```

Bill A shall mirror the substantive economic tax architecture of H.R. 25 to the maximum extent legally and operationally possible as a state-level mirror. Where H.R. 25 already defines an economic classification necessary to FairTax treatment, Engineering shall not invent a separate Kansas definition. Exact operative H.R. 25 language shall be used where the intended Kansas rule is substantively identical; paraphrase that could change meaning is forbidden. Federal-only machinery may be adapted for Kansas jurisdiction/administration only without silently altering the H.R. 25 economic standard.

Existence, ownership, possession, accumulation, or appreciation of wealth (including money, savings, income merely because earned/received, land, real/personal property, investments, and other owned resources) shall **not** create a Kansas FairTax taxable event. Permitted taxable-event architecture is final consumption as defined and controlled by H.R. 25.

Used property, business-purpose, investment-purpose, mixed-use, conversion, and gaming/lottery/chance treatment shall follow H.R. 25. Do not create a separate Kansas FairTax on a chance if H.R. 25 does not treat the corresponding chance as taxable property or a taxable service.

**Expressly identified Kansas deviation (intent):** retained excise taxes **if later Human-authorized after evidenced audit**. Where an excise is retained, Kansas FairTax shall not stack on the same transaction unless the Human Engineer later expressly authorizes stacking. Motor fuel is the current **example of the uniform surviving-excise standard**, not a unique exception class and not a RETAIN decision (Q-BILL-A-003 / §2.4). Business-purpose FairTax treatment does not exempt a surviving excise. Every surviving excise must have an expressly declared bounded purpose; 100% of proceeds restricted to direct purpose execution; no government administrative/overhead charge against the pool. Control chain: EXCISE TAX → EXPRESS PURPOSE → RESTRICTED POOL → DIRECT PURPOSE EXECUTION.

Unused excise balances, sinking funds, rate reductions, refunds, credits, and related disposition mechanisms are **OPEN** — do not invent.

Kansas revenue-claim inventory and REMAIN / TRANSFORM / DISAPPEAR / REQUIRES HUMAN DECISION / EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT classifications are **not invented** by this recording. Architecture is recorded; inventory remains EVIDENCE REQUIRED / OPEN.

**CWC-CE-123 preservation, then Q-BILL-A-003 supersession of candidate-retention framing:** Items A–O in WD-BILL-A-010 §2 remain historical Human Intent except that **motor fuel as a “PRESENT HUMAN CANDIDATE” retained deviation is superseded** by Q-BILL-A-003 (WD-BILL-A-012 / §2.4): motor fuel is the **example of the uniform surviving-excise standard**, not a unique exception class and not a RETAIN decision. Lottery ticket sales remain Human-classified as **not** a Kansas FairTax taxable transaction; H.R. 25 gaming/chances treatment remains on the evidence crosswalk (VERIFY vs IH PDF).
