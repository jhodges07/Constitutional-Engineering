## 2.2 Human Engineering Intent recorded from Q-BILL-A-001 (2026-08-31)

**Classification:** HUMAN INTENT / WORKING DEFINITION.

**Source of record:** `Engineering-Office/definition/working/bill-a/WD-BILL-A-007-Q-BILL-A-001-Human-Intent-Working-Definition.md` (SRC-BILL-A-012).

**Not:** a specification; legislative language; a claim of current Kansas law; HG-D1 acceptance; a maturity change; publication.

The Human Engineer states that the fundamental problem Bill A must solve is that the existing tax architecture can hide, unbound, and invert the proper order of property, signal, claim, pooling, stewardship, and accountability — treating government revenue need as if it created property rights, additional tax sources, unlimited ownership of collected resources, a permanent rate entitlement, and an automatic taxpayer backstop.

Bill A is to be considered structurally successful only if the replacement architecture can establish and protect the objectives listed in WD-BILL-A-007 §14, including at least:

- property rights before government pooling;
- clear signals before revenue claims;
- one ultimate source of taxation — people with money;
- clear separation between tax source and tax collection mechanism;
- visible taxpayer burden;
- explicitly defined taxable events;
- bounded government revenue authority;
- taxpayer ownership as an engineering objective and government stewardship rather than unlimited ownership;
- auditable constitutional spending authority at state and local levels;
- sinking-fund financial discipline;
- non-permanent assumptions about tax rates;
- an end to taxpayers as the automatic backstop for poor planning;
- four-month node accountability with controlled consequences for node failure;
- transparency of collection and expenditure;
- expiration / reauthorization where required;
- enforceable controls against government drift.

Working architectural chain (intent only; architecture remains under Engineering Definition):

```text
PERSON WITH ECONOMIC RESOURCES
→ PROPERTY RIGHT
→ CLEAR GOVERNMENT SIGNAL
→ AUTHORIZED TAXABLE EVENT
→ BOUNDED REVENUE CLAIM
→ COLLECTION / TRANSMISSION MECHANISM
→ GOVERNMENT POOLING
→ TAXPAYER-OWNERSHIP / STEWARDSHIP CONTROL
→ CONSTITUTIONALLY AUTHORIZED SPENDING
→ GOVERNMENT NODE
→ FOUR-MONTH ACCOUNTABILITY
→ PERFORMANCE SIGNAL
→ CONTINUED AUTHORITY / CORRECTIVE ACTION / FAILURE RESPONSE
→ EXPIRATION OR REAUTHORIZATION
```

The Human Engineer expressly reserved legal feasibility of continuing taxpayer ownership after payment, constitutional spending boundaries, tax incidence, legality of taxable events, rates, replacement-revenue quantity, node definition, and node-failure legal consequences as matters requiring research and Human disposition. This LOU records that reservation. It does **not** convert those items into facts.

# 7. Agreed Understanding

**NONE ACCEPTED.** HG-D1 remains NOT PASSED.

The following are **PROPOSED UNDERSTANDING** seeds transcribed from classified Human Intent (Q-BILL-A-001). They are **not** accepted understanding, not requirements, and not Kansas legal findings.

1. Bill A's tax architecture shall start from the person and that person's property, not from the government revenue pool; property rights precede government pooling.  
2. Signal must precede claim: authority, taxable event, obligated person, timing, determination method, destination of funds, limits, and expiration/reconsideration shall be defined before a revenue claim.  
3. There is only one ultimate source of taxation for Bill A design: people with money. Collection mechanisms, legal entities, and tax names do not create additional ultimate sources.  
4. Bill A shall distinguish the ultimate source of taxation from the mechanism used to collect or transmit it. Reducing the number of tax names is not by itself the success condition; visibility of economic reality is.  
5. Taxpayer ownership of tax dollars and government stewardship of tax dollars are central engineering objectives. Payment into a pool shall not automatically be treated as unlimited government ownership. Legal feasibility of continuing ownership after payment is **not** established here.  
6. Current spending / current tax revenue shall not automatically define required replacement revenue. Authorized functions, constitutional obligations, lawful debt, contracts, transition, performance, efficiency, and expenditures lacking continuing authority must be examined first.  
7. Structural success is defined by the objective list in WD-BILL-A-007 §14 and the working architectural chain in §16 of that source. The architecture remains under Engineering Definition.

Prior LOU-001 candidate statements are **not** imported as agreed understanding.
