## 1. Purpose

Provide the smallest controlled structure for the ordinary weekly KSB press release so ChatGPT/phone operators produce consistent public prose without inventing engineering facts.

---

## 2. Authority firewall

```text
CONTROLLED REPOSITORY EVIDENCE
 → CONTROLLED KSB STATUS
 → PRESS RELEASE
```

Never reverse. The press release SHALL NOT change maturity, Bill identity, certification, HG-D1, HG-PR, control status, research acceptance, or Git state.

If evidence does not support a claim: omit it or disclose uncertainty. Do not invent progress.

---

## 3. Word target

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Target | approximately **500** words |
| Controlled tolerance | **450–550** words |
| Counting | ordinary English word count of body prose (exclude YAML/front-matter if any) |

Outside tolerance → editorial follow-up or regenerate; do not falsify facts to pad/trim.

---

## 4. Required structure (flexible prose)

Operators SHOULD include the following sections in order (headings optional in public text; substance required):

1. **Headline** — BlueprintLiberty / Kansas engineering-status framing (not election/polling language).  
2. **Date / context** — controlled public status date (`yyyy.mm.ww`) and calendar date as applicable.  
3. **Opening status statement** — what this weekly KSB Status measures.  
4. **Bill A** — FIXED public title + certified maturity % + brief accurate meaning.  
5. **Bill B** — FIXED public title + certified maturity % + brief accurate meaning.  
6. **Bill C** — FIXED public title + certified maturity % + brief accurate meaning.  
7. **Engineering explanation** — maturity % = controlled engineering development progress, **not** election probability, passage odds, polling, or enactment prediction.  
8. **Current-cycle development** — only material progress/constraints supported by controlled evidence for this cycle.  
9. **Public meaning** — why engineering legislation before asking government to enact it.  
10. **Closing** — project statement + `BlueprintLiberty.com`.

---

## 5. Prohibited language (ordinary)

Do not present percentages as:

- election probabilities;  
- passage probabilities;  
- polling results;  
- political odds;  
- predictions of legislative enactment.

Do not imply HG-D1 acceptance, LOU acceptance, bill introduction, or candidate endorsement unless controlled evidence and Active authority say so.
